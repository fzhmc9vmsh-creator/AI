### 3-6. 会社が公表する FV の変動要因(原文要旨)

- Q2'26: "Personal loan marks were down from the prior quarter, driven by an increase in the weighted average discount rate which was due to a higher benchmark rate, as well as a modest decline in weighted average coupon rate." / 和訳: 個人ローンのマークは、ベンチマーク金利上昇による割引率の上昇と、クーポン小幅低下で前期比低下。(Q2'26 10-Q、T1要約経由)
