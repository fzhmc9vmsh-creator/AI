## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Unisound AI Technology Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, provides artificial intelligence products and solutions in the People's Republic of China. The company offers the UniGPT foundation model and Beast Tooth intelligent agent platform, which provide smart customer service and smart marketing solutions; a one-stop smart rail transit intelligent agent application platform; a Cloud Customer Service system; Swift and Hummingbird AI chips; and speaking evaluation services for residential, commercial, and transportation applications. It also provides smart healthcare solutions, including audio medical transcription solutions, AI in medical record quality management solutions, and other AI-empowered medical solutions to medical institutions, as well as AI software, software-embedded hardware, and AI platform services. In addition, the company engages in the research and development of technology. The company was formerly known as Beijing Yunzhisheng Information Technology Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Unisound AI Technology Co., Ltd. in June 2019. Unisound AI Technology Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2012 and is headquartered in Beijing, the People's Republic of China.
