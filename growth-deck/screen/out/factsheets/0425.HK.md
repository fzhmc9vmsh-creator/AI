## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Minth Group Limited, an investment holding company, designs, develops, manufactures, processes, and sells automobile body parts and moulds of passenger cars. The company manufactures, develops, and sells motor systems for electric vehicles, as well as metal molds, automobile body parts; automobile body parts manufacturing equipment, automation machines, software, production lines, and automobile drives; and offers bookkeeping services. It also engages in the import and export trading, wholesale of packaging materials, logistics, and technology import activities; manufacture and sale of electrical and electronic equipment; production, operation trading, and import and export trade of deformed aluminum alloys, aluminum profiles, and aluminum extrusion moulds; and research, design, development, sales, import, and export of robots. In addition, the company provides automobile glass, light metals casting, customer, research and market development, general business, business management, nursery, internet information, trading, technology service and development, market development, non-ferrous metal smelting, power supply, engineering, management and technical consulting, properties services management, retail sales, and consulting services, as well as act as an agent for sales of automobile body parts and purchase of raw materials. Further, it involved in the design and production of civil aircraft parts; manufacturing of photovoltaic panels; research, development, manufacturing, sales, and after-sales service of energy vehicle drive systems and parts; and manufacture of other parts and accessories for motor vehicles. The company operates in the People's Republic of China, the United States, Japan, Thailand, Germany, Serbia, Mexico, the United Kingdom, the Republic of Korea, France, the Czech Republic, Canada, and Poland. Minth Group Limited was incorporated in 2005 and is headquartered in Taipei City, Taiwan.
