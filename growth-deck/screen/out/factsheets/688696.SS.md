## 事業の説明(yfinance)

XGIMI Technology Co.,Ltd. engages in the research and development, production, and sale of multi-functional smart projectors and laser TVs in China and internationally. The company provides home, portable, and laser projectors. It also offers accessories, such as stands, cases, remotes, cables and adapters, 3D glasses, and other products. The company sells its products through e-commerce platforms and offline channels. The company was incorporated in 2013 and is based in Chengdu, China.
