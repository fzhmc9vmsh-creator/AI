## 事業の説明(yfinance)

China Beststudy Education Group provides after-school education services for K-12 student groups in China. The company provides education related courses, such as full-time test preparation business, talent education, after-school tutoring program, vocational education, and other activities. It is also involved in provision of management consultancy services; publicity services and advertising; internet information services; provision of technical support and development services; and investment activities. China Beststudy Education Group was founded in 1997 and is headquartered in Guangzhou, China.
