## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Sunshine Insurance Group Company Limited provides various insurance products and related services in the People's Republic of China. It operates through three segments: Life Insurance, Property and Casualty Insurance, and Other Businesses segments. The company offers life, health, and accident insurance products; property and casualty insurance products comprising automobile, accident, short-term health, guarantee, liability, agriculture, cargo, and commercial property insurance products. It also provides credit and guarantee insurance, and asset management services. The company distributes through individual insurance, bancassurance, group insurance, telemarketing, online sales, and insurance brokers. The company was formerly known as Sunshine Insurance Holdings Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Sunshine Insurance Group Company Limited in 2007. Sunshine Insurance Group Company Limited was founded in 2005 and is based in Wan Chai, Hong Kong.
