## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shanghai Milkground Food Tech Co., Ltd engages in the manufacture and sale of cheese and liquid milk products to consumers and industrial clients in China. It offers cheese nutrition for kids that include cheese sticks and family cooking cheese, including cream cheese spread, cheese slices, butter, cheese tablets, cheese slices for weight loss, mozzarella cheese, refine mozzarella cheese shreds, baked cheese, whipping cream, and honey fried cheese. The company also engages in dairy product trading products business. It sells its products through online and offline channels, including distributors, e-commerce platforms, snack retailers, supermarkets, catering clients, and instant retail. The company was founded in 1998 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
