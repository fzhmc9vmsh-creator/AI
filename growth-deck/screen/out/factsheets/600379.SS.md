## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shaanxi Baoguang Vacuum Electric Device Co., Ltd. engages in the production and sale of vacuum interrupters in China. The company operates through two segments: Power Equipment and New Energy. The Power Equipment business offers a vacuum interrupter, which is the core component of the power transmission and transformation line interrupter, which is used in mining, smelting, chemical industry, equipment manufacturing, light industry manufacturing, rail transportation, and other industrial and mining enterprises. It also offers metallized ceramics used for the insulating shells of vacuum interrupters. The New Energy business provides EMS software; sells system software and invests in various types of power energy storage assets; power auxiliary services, such as frequency regulation, voltage regulation, peak regulation, standby, and simultaneously; overall solutions for green energy factories; and technical support and other services. The company is also involved in the hydrogen energy business focused on hydrogen production, storage, transportation, and sales; operation of special gases; and solar thermal energy businesses. Shaanxi Baoguang Vacuum Electric Device Co., Ltd. was founded in 1970 and is based in Baoji, China.
