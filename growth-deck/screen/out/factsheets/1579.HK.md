## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Yihai International Holding Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, produces and sells hot pot condiment, compound condiment, and ready-to-eat food products in the People's Republic of China and internationally. The company offers hot pot soup flavorings and hot pot dipping sauces; Chinese-style and Western-style compound condiments, ready-to-eat sauces, chicken powder, and spices; and self-serving small hot pots, instant vermicelli, instant meal, puffed food, and other snacks under the Haidilao and Magic Cook brands. It also sells raw materials for trade and packaged goods. The company was founded in 2005 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
