## 事業の説明(yfinance)

JINHUI LIQUOR Co., Ltd. engages in the production and sale of liquor in China. The company also engages in the manufacture of alcoholic beverages, soft drinks, and refined tea; food production and sale; professional design services; paper product manufacturing; graphic design; and recycled resource sales businesses. It offers its products through distributors, supermarkets, direct sales, and online sales. The company was incorporated in 2009 and is headquartered in Longnan, China.
