## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Sailun Group Co., Ltd. engages in the research and development, manufacturing, and sale of tires and tire recycling products in China, Europe, the United States, Asia, Africa, and internationally. The company offers semi-steel radial tires, all-steel radial tires, and off-road tires that are used in passenger cars, light trucks, large buses, trucks, construction machinery, special vehicles, and other fields; and recycling products, including retreaded tires and materials, tread rubbers, rubber powders, steel wires, etc. It also provides rubber products, machinery and equipment, molds and chemical products, as well as technology software development, installation, and related services. The company offers its products under Sailun, Luji, Black Hawk, Ruiketu, Black Knight, Saiwei, Maichi, and other brands. It also exports its products. The company was formerly known as Sailun Jinyu Group Co., Ltd. Sailun Group Co., Ltd. was founded in 2002 and is based in Qingdao, China.
