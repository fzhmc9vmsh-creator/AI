## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Newborn Town Inc. operates as a social networking entertainment company worldwide. The company engages in the operation of MICO, a live streaming social networking platform; YoHo, an audio social networking platform; SUGO, a companion-based social networking platform; TopTop, a game-oriented social networking platform; LGBTQ+ platforms, including Blued and Heesay; and Finka, a social networking business. It also offers Alice's Dream, a game product; Heer Health for HIV prevention and treatment, and sexual health services; Aippy, a consumer-grade AI application; and advertising spaces to agencies of advertisers. The company was founded in 2009 and is headquartered in Sha Tin, Hong Kong.
