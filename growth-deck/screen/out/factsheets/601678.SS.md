## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Befar Group Co., Ltd engages in manufacturing and sale of chemicals. The company offers products in three sectors, such as chlor-alkali chemicals, including sodium hydroxide, chlorohydrin process propylene oxide, epichlorohydrin, allyl chloride, trichloroethylene, perchloroethylene, and other chlor-alkali chemicals; C3-C4 chemicals, such as methyl tert-butyl ether (MTBE), co-oxidation process propylene oxide, propane, propylene, and other C3-C4 chemicals; and wet electronic chemicals, including electronic grade hydrofluoric acid. It also engages in sale of electricity and steam energy; and offers research and development services. In addition, it provides product applications, include chemicals engineering, dyeing and printing, chemical fibers, water treatment, paper manufacturing, lithium battery, pharmaceuticals, metal processing, refrigeration, pesticides, cleaning, and etching. Befar Group Co.,Ltd was founded in 1968 and is headquartered in Binzhou, China.
