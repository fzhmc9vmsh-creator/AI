## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Great Microwave Technology Co., Ltd. engages in the research, development, production, and sale of integrated circuit chips and microsystems in China. The company offers radio frequency front-end and transceiver chips; high-speed, high-precision ADC/DAC chips; power management chips; and microsystems and modules. It also provides technical services. The company's products and technologies are used in various industries, such as data links, electronic countermeasures, wireless communication terminals, next-generation radios, phased array communications, phased array radars, and power supply and distribution for electronic systems. Great Microwave Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in 2015 and is headquartered in Hangzhou, China.
