## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Medcaptain Medical Technology Co., Ltd. manufactures integrated perioperative medical devices in China, Europe, Asia, the Middle East, Africa, North America, and South America. It offers life support products, such as infusion systems, medication delivery products, anesthesia machines and vaporizers, workstations, imaging equipment, and other life support products, including airway management, pulmonary function testing devices, deep vein thrombosis (DVT) preventive pumps, vein illuminators, monitors, and veterinary life support products; minimally invasive intervention products, such as an endoscopic product portfolio for the diagnosis and treatment of conditions relating to the digestive system, including the gastrointestinal (GI) and hepatobiliary tracts, and the urological system; and In Vitro diagnostics products, such as thromboelastography analyzers, blood grouping analyzers, chemiluminescent immunoassay (CLIA) testing analyzers and reagent kits, and molecular diagnostics equipment and testing kits. The company sells its products through a chain of distributors globally. Medcaptain Medical Technology Co., Ltd. was formerly known as Shenzhen Yusheng Medical Technology Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Medcaptain Medical Technology Co., Ltd. in July 2023. Medcaptain Medical Technology Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2011 and is based in Shenzhen, China.
