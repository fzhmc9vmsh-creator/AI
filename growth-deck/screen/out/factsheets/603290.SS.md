## 事業の説明(yfinance)

StarPower Semiconductor Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, design, development, production, and sale of power semiconductor chips and modules in Asia and internationally. The company offers IGBT, SiC, IPM, MOSFET, FRD, and diode modules, as well as discrete devices and customized modules. Its products are used in industrial control applications, including low voltage inverters, medium and high voltage inverters, and welding machines; automotive electronics applications consisting of main and auxiliary drives; renewable energy applications, such as photovoltaic power generation, SVG/APF, wind power, power, and energy storage systems; and household appliances, including white goods and commercial air conditioning. StarPower Semiconductor Ltd. was founded in 2005 and is headquartered in Jiaxing, China.
