## 事業の説明(yfinance)

LITALICO Inc., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the employment support, child welfare, platform, and overseas businesses in Japan and the United States. The company offers LITALICO Junior, a preschool and learning support classes for children with developmental and learning disabilities; LITALICO Wonder, an IT and manufacturing classroom; LITALICO Life, a life planning consultation and workshop; LITALICO Development Navi, a developmental disability portal site; LITALICO Developmental Characteristics Test, an online personality assessment service; and LITALICO High School, a correspondence high school support school. It also provides LITALICO Works, a job transition support service for people with disabilities seeking employment; LITALICO Terrace, an independent living training program and support service; LITALICO Job Navi, a job information site for people with disabilities; LITALICO Residences, a group homes for disabled individuals; and LITALICO Educational Software, a support service for teachers involved in special needs education. The company was founded in 2005 and is headquartered in Meguro Japan.
