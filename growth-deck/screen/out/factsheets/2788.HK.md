## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Chuangxin Industries Holdings Limited produces and sells electrolytic aluminum and alumina in the People's Republic of China. The company engages in alumina refining and electrolytic aluminum smelting within the upstream of the aluminum industry chain. Its electrolytic aluminum products primarily include liquid aluminum and aluminum ingots. It is also involved in the commodity trading platform; smelting and selling electrolytic aluminum; wind power generation; recycling and processing metals; manufacturing and selling eco-environmental materials; designing and researching of metal materials; and business aviation charter services. In addition, the company offers alumina and other related products; coal and non-ferrous metals; scrap and other materials; steam supply; and electricity transmission services. It serves 3C electronics, lightweight automotive, green energy, transportation, industrial materials, and construction industries. Chuangxin Industries Holdings Limited was incorporated in 2023 and is based in Tongliao, China. Chuangxin Industries Holdings Limited operates as a subsidiary of Bloomsbury Holding Limited.
