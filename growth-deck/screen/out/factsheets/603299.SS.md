## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Jiang Su Suyan Jingshen Co.,Ltd. engages in the mining, research, production, distribution, and sale of salt and salt chemicals in China. The company offers two-alkali and small industrial salt, edible salts, soda ash, calcium chloride, baking soda, and other products. The company also offers able salt, industrial salt for alkali production, sodium sulfate, small-scale industrial salt, packaging bags, and brine. The company was formerly known as Jiangsu Jingshen Salt & Chemical Industry Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Jiang Su Suyan Jingshen Co.,Ltd. in January 2019. Jiang Su Suyan Jingshen Co.,Ltd. was founded in 2001 and is based in Huai'an, China. Jiang Su Suyan Jingshen Co.,Ltd. operates as a subsidiary of Jiangsu Salt Industry Group Co.,Ltd.
