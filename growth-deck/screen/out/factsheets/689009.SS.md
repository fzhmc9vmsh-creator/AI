## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Ninebot Limited engages in the research and development, production, sale, and servicing of short-distance transportation and robot products in China and internationally. It offers electric balance scooters, electric scooters, electric motorcycles, all-terrain vehicles, go-karts, and service robots. The company was founded in 2012 and is based in Beijing, China.
