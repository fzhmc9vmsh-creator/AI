## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Centuri Holdings, Inc. operates as a utility infrastructure services company in North America. It operates through four segments: U.S. Gas Utility Services; Canadian Operations; Union Electric Utility Services; and Non-Union Electric Utility Services. The company offers gas utility services, including maintenance, replacement, repair, and installation for local natural gas distribution utilities focused on the modernization of customers' infrastructure. It also provides electric utility services encompassing maintenance, replacement, repair, upgrade, and expansion services for urban transmission and local distribution infrastructure, as well as corporate and non-allocated transactions. The company customers include electric, gas, and combination utility providers, as well as serves end markets, such as distributed power projects, and data centers. The company was founded in 1909 and is headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona.
