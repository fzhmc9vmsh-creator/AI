## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Phancy Group Co., Ltd., an investment holding company, engages in operations of artificial intelligence (AI) platform in the People's Republic of China. The company offers 4ParadigmSage Platform, an enterprise Al business system; 4Paradigm SHIFT, a standardized solutions built on the Sage AI Platform, tailored to different industry business scenarios; and 4Paradigm AIGS, an AI software development platform. It is also involved in the research and development of technology; investment activities; provision of artificial intelligent platform and solutions in energy and power and manufacturing industries; and provision of digital operation and maintenance platform and solutions. The company was formerly known as Beijing Fourth Paradigm Technology Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Phancy Group Co., Ltd. in January 2026. Phancy Group Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2014 and is headquartered in Beijing, China.
