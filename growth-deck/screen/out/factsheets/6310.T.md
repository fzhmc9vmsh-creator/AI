## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Iseki & Co., Ltd. engages in the development, manufacture, and sale of agricultural machinery in Japan, Asia, Europe, the United States, and internationally. It offers cultivating machineries, including tractors, tillers, high clearance multipurpose vehicles, and mowers; planting machinery, such as rice and vegetable transplanters; and harvesting machinery comprising combine harvesters and binders. The company also provides processing machinery, such as rice hullers, dryers, rice cleaners, rice graders, vegetable harvesting, and processing machinery; and implements, spare parts, agricultural facilities, as well as diesel engine. Iseki & Co., Ltd. was founded in 1926 and is headquartered in Arakawa, Japan.
