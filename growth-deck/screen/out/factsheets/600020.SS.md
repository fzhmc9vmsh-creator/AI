## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Henan Zhongyuan Expressway Company Limited engages in investment, construction, operation, and management of expressways in China. It operates through Highway Toll Road and Bridge Division; Real Estate Development Division; Investment Business Division; and Others segments. The company operates Airport Expressway, the Zhengzhou-Zhumadian section of the Beijing-Hong Kong-Macau Expressway, the Zhengzhou-Yaoshan section of the Zhengluan-Luan Expressway, Zhengzhou-Minquan Expressway, Shangqiu-Dengfeng Expressway, and Yongcheng section of Dezhou-Shangqiu Expressway. It is also involved in the project investment; investment management, and consulting business; real estate development and sales; software sales and technical services; civil engineering construction; housing rental; venture capital, human resources, and technology promotion and application; and property management businesses. Henan Zhongyuan Expressway Company Limited was founded in 2000 and is based in Zhengzhou, China.
