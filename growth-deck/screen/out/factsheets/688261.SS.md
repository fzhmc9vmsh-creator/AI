## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Suzhou Oriental Semiconductor Company Limited, a semiconductor company, engages in the research, development, manufacture, and sale of power devices, digital power management ICs, digital real-time controllers, and automotive-grade analog front-end ICs in China and internationally. The company offers SJ and SGT metal-oxide-semiconductor field-effect transistors (MOSFETs), insulated-gate bipolar transistors, trench MOSFETs, power modules, SiC MOSFETs, gallium nitride high electron mobility transistors, power ICs, low dropout products, operational amplifiers, and DSPs. Its products are used in data center computing power infrastructure, robotics power and motor drive systems, industrial and communication power supplies, on-board chargers, DC charging piles, photovoltaic inverters, and energy storage systems. Suzhou Oriental Semiconductor Company Limited was incorporated in 2008 and is headquartered in Suzhou, China.
