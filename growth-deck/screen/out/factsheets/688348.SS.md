## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Yuneng Technology Co., Ltd. engaged in the research and development and industrialization of component-level power electronics technology and energy storage technology in China and internationally. The company offers microinverters and energy communication, off-grid hybrid inverter, and intelligent shutdown systems. It also provides equipment products, AI-powered smart energy services, and distributed photovoltaic power station services; and residential, industrial, and commercial photovoltaic and energy storage systems. The company was incorporated in 2010 and is headquartered in Jiaxing, China.
