## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Finatext Holdings Ltd. engages in the fintech solution, big data analysis, and financial infrastructure businesses in Japan. The company provides Cloud-native and API based financial infrastructure. It also develops front-end applications, such as mobile apps. In addition, it offers financial education, demo trading, and other services through website and smartphone apps. Further, the company provides data to the financial and real estate industries, and data and artificial intelligence solutions. Finatext Holdings Ltd. was incorporated in 2013 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
