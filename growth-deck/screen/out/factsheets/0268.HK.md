## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Kingdee International Software Group Company Limited, an investment holding company, engages in the subscription and sale of software worldwide. It also provides subscription and software implementation services, software solution consulting, maintenance, upgrade and other supporting services, and other services. In addition, the company designs, develops, manufactures, and sells software and hardware products; and provides software-related and online financial intermediation services. It serves enterprises, government agencies, and other organizations. The company was founded in 1993 and is headquartered in Shenzhen, the People's Republic of China.
