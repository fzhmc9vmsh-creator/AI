## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Hubei Sinophorus Electronic Materials Co.,Ltd. engages in the research and development, production, and sale of electronic chemicals in China and internationally. The company's products include general wet electronic chemicals, such as electronic-grade phosphoric, sulfuric acid, hydrogen peroxide; and functional wet electronic chemicals comprising etching solutions, and cleaning agents. Hubei Sinophorus Electronic Materials Co.,Ltd. was founded in 2008 and is headquartered in Yichang, China.
