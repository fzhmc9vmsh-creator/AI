## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Jiangsu Zenergy Battery Technologies Group Co., Ltd. engages in research, development, production, and sale of EV battery products, ESS battery products, and aviation battery products. The company offers battery solutions, such as battery cells, modules, packs, racks, and battery management systems. It serves the passenger vehicle power battery markets. The company was formerly known as Jiangsu Zenergy Battery Technologies Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Jiangsu Zenergy Battery Technologies Group Co., Ltd. in July 2024. Jiangsu Zenergy Battery Technologies Group Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2019 and is headquartered in Changshu, China.
