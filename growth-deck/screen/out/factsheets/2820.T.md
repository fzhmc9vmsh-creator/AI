## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Yamami Company manufactures and sells tofu in Japan. The company offers cotton, silken, filling, grilled, deep fried, deep fried, shredded fried, and commercial tofu, as well as okara powder. It also provides soybeans and cotton noodles. The company was incorporated in 1975 and is headquartered in Mihara, Japan.
