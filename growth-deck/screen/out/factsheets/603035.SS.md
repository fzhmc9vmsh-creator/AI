## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Jiangsu Changshu Automotive Trim Group Co., Ltd. engages in the research and development, production, and sales of automotive interior parts assembly products in China and internationally. The company provides door panel assemblies, instrument panel assemblies, sub-instrument panel assemblies, pillar assemblies, trunk assemblies, coat rack assemblies, and underbody panels, as well as molds, inspection tools, and automated equipment design and manufacturing. The company was formerly known as Changshu Automotive Trim Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Jiangsu Changshu Automotive Trim Group Co., Ltd. In September 2020. The company was founded in 1992 and is based in Changshu, China.
