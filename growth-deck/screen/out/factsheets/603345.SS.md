## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Anjoy Foods Group Co., Ltd. engages in the research, development, processing, production, wholesaling, retailing, and sale of quick-frozen food products in China. It sells frozen food, including quick-frozen prepared food, quick-frozen rice and flour products, and quick-frozen prepared dishes. The company offers quick-frozen flavored and processed food such as stuffed meatballs, fish tofu, fish roe buns, and pork sausage under the Anjoy brand. Its quick-frozen prepared products include dishes such as crayfish, shrimp paste, fried crispy strips, beef and lamb rolls, Qianye tofu, and egg dumplings. It also provides quick-frozen flour and rice products such as shumai, steamed buns, Chinese pancakes, and brown sugar buns; frozen baked goods such as toast, pineapple buns, scones, soft buns, and bagels; and ready-to-heat and ready-to-cook products. In addition, the company offers hot pot and barbecue food, semi-finished food, and rice and flour products. Further, it sells its products through distributors, targeted enterprises and other institutional customers, new retail channels, e-commerce platforms, and supermarkets. Anjoy Foods Group Co., Ltd. was founded in 2001 and is headquartered in Xiamen, the People's Republic of China.
