## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Circuit Fabology Microelectronics Equipment Co., Ltd. engages in the research, development, manufacture, sales, and maintenance of direct imaging equipment and direct writing lithography equipment in Chinese Mainland, Thailand, Japan, Taiwan China, and worldwide. It offers PCB direct imaging equipment and automation system, used in the lithography process for the manufacturing of PCBs, lithography of pattern layers, and solder mask layers; semiconductor direct-writing lithography equipment and automation system, used in the lithography process for IC mask fabrication, IC substrate, and advanced packaging and micro/mini LED and OLED display panel production; and after-sales maintenance and support services. It sells its products through distributors. Circuit Fabology Microelectronics Equipment Co., Ltd. was founded in 2015 and is headquartered in Hefei, China.
