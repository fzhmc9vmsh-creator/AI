## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Arctech Solar Holding Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and supplies integrated photovoltaic power generation system solutions and products in China and internationally. The company offers solar trackers; fixed-tilt structures; flexible PV mounting systems; PV cleaning robots; utility-scale, commercial, and industrial energy storage; balcony solar kits and building integrated photovoltaics; and mobile solar microgrids. It also provides agrivoltaics, terrain-adaptive, mitigate extreme weather risk, module compatibility, and smart manufacturing solutions. In addition, the company engages in the manufacturing and sale of photovoltaic brackets, stereo garages, reducers, special equipment, and other related materials and components; emerging energy technology research and development; technical services; photovoltaic power development and consulting; and investment management. It exports its products. The company was founded in 2009 and is headquartered in Kunshan, China.
