## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shandong Donghong Pipe Industry Co., Ltd. engages in engineering pipeline business in China. The company offers pipelines, such as SRTP, polyethylene pipes, PVC pipes, insulation pipes, and anti-corrosion metal pipes, as well as pipe connections; financing services, pipeline construction and installation services, and after-sales services; and pipeline engineering design, material procurement, product research and development, product manufacturing, engineering construction, and project operation and maintenance. It is also involved in the manufacturing of engineering pipeline products for use in municipal, water conservancy, industrial and mining, thermal, and other large-scale projects. The company was founded in 1997 and is headquartered in Qufu, China. Shandong Donghong Pipe Industry Co., Ltd. operates as a subsidiary of Shandong Donghong Group Co., Ltd.
