## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Lung Fung Group Holdings Limited, through its subsidiaries, sells beauty, health and pharmaceutical products. The company offers Chinese and western medicines, health products, medical supplies, beauty and skin care, cosmetics, maternal and infant products, food, and personal care, and household daily necessities. It is also involved in advertising and promotion agencies; operation of retail stores; and wholesaling activities. The company sells its products through retail stores and online sale platforms. The company was founded in 1992 and is headquartered in Fanling, Hong Kong. Lung Fung Group Holdings Limited operates as a subsidiary of TTK Holding Limited.
