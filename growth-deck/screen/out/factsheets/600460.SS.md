## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Hangzhou Silan Microelectronics Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, design, development, production, and sale of electronic components in China. It offers discrete devices, including LVMOS, HVMOS, IGBT, SiC, and diode; IPM products; industrial and automobile IGBT modules; AC-DC and DC-DC converters; fast charging ICs; and LED lighting drivers, such as dimming and non-dimming LED lighting, LED headlight, and automotive low-voltage motor drivers, as well as smart power switches. The company also provides three-phase and singe half bridge drivers, as well as PFC IGBT gate driver; MEMS sensor comprising triaxial acceleration, six-axis inertial, proximity detection and digital ambient light, heart rate, and silicon microphone sensors; MCU IC products; and digital audio/video, which includes CD SERVO, audio decode, audio effects processing, intelligent audio. In addition, it offers ASIC products, such as bipolar PWM controller and DC-DC, LDO, logic and switch and level shift, comparator, reference IC, and linear regulator, as well as volume and sound control, DC drive, darlington, operational amplifier, and audio circuits; and GaN and GaAs LED chips. Further, the company is involved in information transmission; software and information technology services; wholesale and retail trade; leasing and business services; and scientific research and technical services. Its products are used in home appliances, industrial, LED lighting and chip, automotive, consumer, and audio-visual applications. The company was incorporated in 1997 and is headquartered in Hangzhou, China.
