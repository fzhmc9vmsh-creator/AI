## 事業の説明(yfinance)

SBT Ultrasonic Technology Co., Ltd. engages in the research and development, design, production, and sale of ultrasonic equipment and accessories internationally. It provides ultrasonic application and intelligent equipment solutions. The company also offers ultrasonic wedge welding machines, integrated ultrasonic welding monitoring machines, ultrasonic roll welding machines, wire harness welding, semiconductor ultrasonic bonding and pin welding machines, ultrasonic die bonders, tire cutting systems, plastic welding machines, and advanced ultrasonic scanning microscopes, as well as related accessories including welding heads, non-woven fabric welding, bottom molds, generators, and transducers. The company products are primarily used in new energy batteries, wire harness connectors, semiconductors, rubber tires, non-woven fabrics, and various high-precision applications in automotive, aerospace, medical, and scientific research industries. It serves customers in the new energy, automotive, semiconductor, tire, consumer electronics, home appliance, medical, and scientific research sectors. SBT Ultrasonic Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in 2007 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
