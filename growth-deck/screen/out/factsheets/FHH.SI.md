## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Foundation Healthcare Holdings Limited designs and develops integrated private healthcare platform that connects medical specialists, healthcare providers, payors, patients, and facilities in Singapore. The company's platform offers three core verticals, including Specialists that provides specialist medical services; Medical Centres, comprising of surgery centres, radiology and imaging centre, and fertility centre; and Technology vertical offers AVA, a platform to connect various stakeholders within the healthcare ecosystem. The company was incorporated in 2022 and is based in Singapore.
