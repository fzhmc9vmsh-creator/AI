## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Furukawa Electric Co., Ltd. provides a wide range of products in the business fields of Optical Solutions, Digital Infrastructure Components, Energy Infrastructure, Automotive Systems and Metal Solutions worldwide. It operates through Infrastructure; Electrical and Electronic; Functional Products; and Services and Developments segments. The company offers optical fiber/optical active components, optical fiber cable, optical closure/optical termination box, optical connector and cord, optical fiber identifier, fusion splicer/optical connecting tool,  broadband system, router/network equipment, industrial infrastructure systems, and wireless products; tape for semiconductor process, cable conduit materials, heat insulator, fluorescent lamp and LED reflectors, polyethylene pipe, heat dissipation/cooling products, aluminum blank material for hard disk drives, copper foil, and optical devices and components; and power cable, cable-related equipment/connection and terminal products, industrial machine-related equipment, power distribution products, metal communication cable, wiring materials, fire prevention products, and submarine water pipe. It also provides wire harness, sliding door harness, BUS harness, corrosion-proof terminal for aluminum wire, connector for automobile, steering roll connector, metal core printed-circuit-board, and lead battery status detection sensor; copper alloy wire/oxygen-free copper wire, insulated and special wires, copper and copper alloy products, plating, and superconducting cable; as well as insulated lasers, infrastructure maintenance, optical medical device development platform, optical fiber probe, optical fiber sensor for diagnostic applications, and detection solutions for internal medical devices. Furukawa Electric Co., Ltd. was founded in 1884 and is headquartered in Chiyoda, Japan.
