## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Fujimi Incorporated manufactures and sells synthetic precision abrasives in Japan, North America, Asia, and Europe. The company offers various products for slicing crystal silicon ingots, lapping, and polishing silicon wafers; abrasives for use in the production of semiconductor devices, such as CMP slurry and other materials; and polishing products for magnetic disks of the hard disk drives. It also provides polishing solutions, including lapping, polishing, and diamond products, as well as polishing compounds for use in electronic components, lighting and LED products, automobiles, and lenses industries. In addition, the company offers thermal spray materials for use in aircraft, FDPs and semiconductors, steel, and energy industries. Further, the company offers specialty materials, such as silicon carbide, titanium phosphate, cemented carbide powder, rod-shaped zinc oxide, and alumina. Fujimi Incorporated was founded in 1950 and is headquartered in Kiyosu, Japan.
