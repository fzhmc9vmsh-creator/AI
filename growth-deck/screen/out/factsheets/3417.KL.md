## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Eastern & Oriental Berhad, an investment holding company, invests in, develops, manages, and sells residential and commercial properties in Malaysia and the United Kingdom. The company operates in three segments: Properties, Hospitality, and Investments and Others. It is also involved in the management and operation of hotels, serviced apartments, and restaurants; and property development and investment activities. In addition, the company provides sales and marketing services; and management and project management services. Eastern & Oriental Berhad was founded in 1885 and is headquartered in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.
