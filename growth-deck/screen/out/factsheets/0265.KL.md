## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Infomina Berhad, an investment holding company, provides technology application and infrastructure solutions in Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, the Philippines, Indonesia, Hong Kong, and Japan. It operates through two segments: Technology Infrastructure Operations, Maintenance, and Support Services; and Design and Delivery of Technology Infrastructure Solutions. The company offers infrastructure and environment management solutions, such as intelligent data center and hybrid infrastructure; security, compliance, and resilience management; application and platform management services; and managed infrastructure services. It also provides application and platform modernisation, cloud migration and hybrid enablement, devops and agile enablement, and integration and connectivity modernisation; AI engineering and machine learning, cognitive automation and process intelligence, advanced analytics and business intelligence, AI-enhanced industry, and customer and experience ecosystems; and consulting and strategic enablement solutions, including IT strategy and roadmap development, enterprise architecture and governance, change management and digital adoption, and training and capability building. In addition, the company is involved in the provision of maintenance and support services for information technologies; technology hardware, software, consultancy and support services; import and export of computer software and hardware; computer programming and computer facility management activities; data management, storage and archiving, reselling of software and software integration; and software designing, development, customisation, implementation, maintenance, testing and benchmarking, designing, developing, dealing in artificial intelligence, and related computer software and solutions. Infomina Berhad was incorporated in 2007 and is based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Infomina Berhad is a subsidiary of Infomina Holdings Sdn Bhd.
