## 事業の説明(yfinance)

BaTeLab Co., Ltd. engages in the research, development, and sale of analog integrated circuit patterned wafers in China. The company offers power management, signal chain, and electronic component products. Its products are used in industrial automation, IoT, and lighting, as well as automotive electronics, healthcare, instrumentation, communications, power, energy storage, and consumer electronics applications. The company was incorporated in 2010 and is headquartered in Suzhou, China.
