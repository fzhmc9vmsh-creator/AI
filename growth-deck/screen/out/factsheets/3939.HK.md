## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Wanguo Gold Group Limited, an investment holding company, engages in mining, ore processing, and sale of concentrate products in the People's Republic of China and Solomon Islands. The company offers copper concentrates, zinc concentrates, iron concentrates, sulfur concentrates, and lead concentrates, and gold and silver by-products. The company was formerly known as Wanguo International Mining Group Limited and changed its name to Wanguo Gold Group Limited in September 2024. Wanguo Gold Group Limited was incorporated in 2011 and is headquartered in Yichun, the People's Republic of China.
