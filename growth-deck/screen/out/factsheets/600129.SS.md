## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Chongqing Taiji Industry(Group) Co.,Ltd, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the production and sales of Chinese and western medicine products in China and internationally. It operates through Pharmaceutical Industry; Pharmaceutical Business; Chinese medicinal materials Resources; Big Health and International; and Service Industry and Others segments. The company offers respiratory system, digestive system and metabolic, nervous system, cardiovascular and cerebrovascular, big health, anti-infective, anti-tumor, immunomodulatory drugs; and anti-aging products. It also engages in accommodation, loading and unloading, warehousing, catering, retail, agriculture, wholesale, and aquaculture activities; and research and development of medicinal herb planting. Chongqing Taiji Industry(Group) Co.,Ltd was incorporated in 1979 and is headquartered in Chongqing, China.
