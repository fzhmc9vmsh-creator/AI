## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Asahi Yukizai Corporation, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the pipe and fittings system, resin, and water treatment and resource development businesses in Japan, the United States, and internationally. It offers manual and automatic valves, as well as plastic pipes and fittings under the ASAHIAV brand; plastic valves under the Dymatrix brand; flow control systems under the Falconics brand; mixers under the Mixworx brand; and engineering services, including tank design and manufacturing, resin processed product manufacturing, structural and piping stress analysis, and supervisor dispatch, as well as piping design and installation, prefabrication, and checks. The company also provides phenolic resins for casting processes, industrial fields, and photoresists; resin coated sands for casting processes; and urethane insulating materials for housing, construction, and civil engineering sectors. In addition, it is involved in the geological survey consulting, well drilling, and geothermal and hot spring development; design and construction of drinking, industrial, gray, and recycled water, as well as household wastewater; industrial water and wastewater treatment; and maintenance and management services. The company was formerly known as Asahi Organic Chemicals Industry Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Asahi Yukizai Corporation in April 2016. Asahi Yukizai Corporation was incorporated in 1945 and is headquartered in Taito, Japan.
