## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Hangzhou Honghua Digital Technology Stock Company LTD. engages in the research and development, production, and sale of digital printing equipment and consumables in China. It provides digital direct-to-garment printers, digital inkjet transfer printers, ultra-high-speed industrial inkjet printers, automated sewing equipment, inkjet rotary digital printers, and supporting consumables inks. It also exports its products. Hangzhou Honghua Digital Technology Stock Company LTD. was founded in 1992 and is headquartered in Hangzhou, China.
