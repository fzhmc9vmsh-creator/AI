## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Rorze Corporation engages in the design, development, manufacture, and sale of automation systems for the semiconductor and flat panel display production in Japan, Taiwan, China, South Korea, the United States of America, France, and internationally. The company provides atmospheric wafer transfer equipment, vacuum wafer transfer equipment, wafer transfer units, robots, aligners, load ports, EFEMs, wafer sorters, N2 purging compatible wafer stockers, control systems, stepping motor drivers, and controllers. It also offers fully automated vapor phase decomposition equipment, large glass substrate transport devices, glass cutting machines, and automation equipment for flat panel display manufacturing. In addition, the company provides incubators and cell culture devices, including Cell Keeper and SCALE48, and related life science automation equipment. In addition, the company provides automation systems, maintenance services, semiconductor manufacturing equipment components, life science equipment, and research contract services related to tissue staining and gene function analysis.  The company was incorporated in 1966 and is based in Fukuyama, Japan.
