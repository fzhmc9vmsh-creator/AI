## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Zhejiang Jiaao Enprotech Stock Co., Ltd, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, development, production, and sale of environmentally friendly plasticizers and stabilizers in China and internationally. It also provides biodiesel and additives. The company's products are used in artificial leather, general PVC products, toys and medical PVC products, cable and electrical applications, synthetic rubber and other resins, food and medical packaging, furniture and decorations, and other applications. In addition, it engages in trading and port operation activities. Zhejiang Jiaao Enprotech Stock Co., Ltd was founded in 2003 and is based in Jiaxing, China.
