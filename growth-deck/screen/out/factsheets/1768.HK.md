## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Busy Ming Group Co., Ltd., through its subsidiaries, operates as a food and beverage retailer in China. The company offers bakery and confectionery products, biscuits, nuts and seeds, puffed snacks and instant meals, chocolates and preserves, and beverages. It sells its products through self-operated and franchised stores under the Busy for You and Super Ming brand names. Busy Ming Group Co., Ltd. was founded in 2016 and is headquartered in Changsha, China.
