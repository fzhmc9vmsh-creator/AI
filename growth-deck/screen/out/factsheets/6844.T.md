## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shindengen Electric Manufacturing Co.,Ltd. manufactures and sells semiconductors, electrical equipment, and power supply products in Japan. It operates in three segments: Power Device Business, Power Unit Business, and Power Systems Business. The company offers DC quick charger, AC charger for EV, wireless charging system, engine and motor control, battery power supply, damper, power steering, and lighting control products; and energy storage systems ad inverters, electrical storage inverters, inverter units, PV inverters, monitoring systems, O&M service, and other products. It also provides industrial equipment, LED lighting, AC/DC power supplies, internet infrastructure, data centers, telecommunication power supplies, and semiconductor power. In addition, it serves the mobility, energy, industrial equipment, telecommunication, and home appliance markets. Shindengen Electric Manufacturing Co.,Ltd. was incorporated in 1949 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
