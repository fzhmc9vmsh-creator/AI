## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Sansan, Inc. engages in the planning, development, and selling of cloud-based solutions in Japan. The company offers Sansan, a cloud-based solution that manages business cards and contacts, andonline activity, as well as meeting history, enabling the sharing of this data companywide to increase net sales while reducing costs; Bill One, a cloud-based solution to redefine the invoices; Contract One, a contract management AX solution; Eight, an app to share virtual card with anyone; and Sansan Data Intelligence, a data quality management solution. Sansan, Inc. was incorporated in 2007 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
