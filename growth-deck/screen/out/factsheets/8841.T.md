## 事業の説明(yfinance)

TOC Co., Ltd. engages in the real estate business in Japan. It operates through the Real Estate Business and the Linen Supply and Laundry Business segments. The company is involved in the leasing, management, and operation of rental showrooms, exhibition halls, conference rooms, and parking lots; rental and cleaning of various textile products such as yukata and sheets, as well as hotel, restaurant, and sports linens; and provision of laundry services. It also engages in the pharmaceutical business, which involves manufacture and sale of gastrointestinal medicines and health foods; sports club and hot spring facility businesses; building management related services provides interior construction work, vending machines, and other equipment; insurance agency business; and information processing related business. The company was founded in 1911 and is headquartered in Shinagawa, Japan.
