## 事業の説明(yfinance)

AEON Financial Service Co., Ltd., through its subsidiaries, provides various financial services in Japan and internationally. The company engages in banking, credit card, loan, life insurance sales, and non-life insurance agency businesses. It also provides short-term small amount insurance and housing related loans; management, collection and purchase of specified monetary claims, backup servicing; digital solutions for local governments; and credit card purchase contracts. In addition, the company is involved in e-money business; and call center operations. AEON Financial Service Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1981 and is based in Chiyoda, Japan.
