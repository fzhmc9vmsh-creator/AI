## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Polaris Holdings Co., Ltd. operates a chain of hotels in Japan. It operates through the Hotel Management Business and Hotel Investment Business segments. The company operates its hotels under the KOKO HOTEL, Best Western, Value The Hotel, Hotel Wing, and Red Planet Hotels brand names, as well as engages in franchise activities. It also provides investment in real estate properties. The company was formerly known as KachikaiHatsu Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Polaris Holdings Co., Ltd. in March 2021. Polaris Holdings Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1912 and is based in Chuo, Japan.
