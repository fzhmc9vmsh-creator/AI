## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Yamaguchi Financial Group, Inc., together with its subsidiaries, provides various banking products and services in Japan. The company engages in securities brokerage, credit card, leasing, regional research and consulting, insurance agent, debt management and collection, investment management, fund management, employee benefits agency, welfare and benefits outsourcing services, and credit guarantee businesses. It is also involved in the agriculture and crowdfunding business, business growth and problem-solving support services, as well as wholesale and retail of agricultural, forestry, and fishery products. Yamaguchi Financial Group, Inc. was incorporated in 2006 and is headquartered in Shimonoseki, Japan.
