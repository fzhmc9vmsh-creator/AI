## 事業の説明(yfinance)

SHIFT Inc. provides software quality assurance and testing solutions in Japan. The company offers CAT, a software test management tool to evaluate software testing aptitude; a quality platform; and software consulting services. The company also engages in the nursery business; support services for the employment, retention, and active participation of people with disabilities; and planning, development, and sales of products and services that contribute to solving social issues. SHIFT Inc. was incorporated in 2005 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
