## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Jiangsu Hengshun Vinegar-Industry Co.,Ltd, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, development, production, and sale of condiments in China. The company offers vinegar, soy sauce, pickles, compound seasonings, and flavor agents. It is also involved in production and sale of alcohol; and food business, and online food sales and related businesses; wholesale and retail trade; leasing and business services; and provision of information transmission, software, and information technology services; and investment activities. The company was founded in 1840 and is headquartered in Zhenjiang, China.
