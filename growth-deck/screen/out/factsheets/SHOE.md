## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shoe Station Group Inc., together with its subsidiaries, retails footwear in the United States. Its products include shoes, sneakers, heels, sandals, boots, work and safety shoes, and athletic shoes; and accessories for men, women and kids. It sells its products through stores, online, and mobile app. The company was formerly known as Shoe Carnival, Inc. and changed its name to Shoe Station Group Inc. in June 2026. Shoe Station Group Inc. was founded in 1978 and is headquartered in Fort Mill, South Carolina.
