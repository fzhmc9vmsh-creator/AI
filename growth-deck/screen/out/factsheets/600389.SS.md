## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Nantong Jiangshan Agrochemical & Chemicals Co.,Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, development, production, and sale of pesticide products in China and internationally. The company offers herbicides, including glyphosate, alachlor, acetochlor, butachlor, butachlor+propanil, quizalofop-p-ethyl, and metolachlor; insecticides, such as dichlorvos, trichlorphon, diazinon, nitenpyram, and imidaclothiz; and fungicides comprising prochloraz and prochloraz manganese complex. It also provides chemical intermediates, which include chloral, phosphorous acid and trichloride, trimethyl and dimethyl phosphites, PMIDA, paraformaldehyde, chloromethane, phosphorus trichloride, phosphorus oxychloride, trichloroacetaldehyde, and chloromethane; and new materials, such as microporous insulating board, nanometer aluminum oxide, flame retardants, water treatment agents, and nanomaterials. In addition, the company offers chlor-alkali; cogeneration steam; basic chemical products comprising caustic soda, chlorine, hydrogen, high-purity hydrochloric acid, and drinking water-grade sodium hypochlorite; and other technical products and formulations of trichlorfon, chlorpyrifos, pyrimethanil, including emulsifiable concentrate, aqueous, soluble concentrates, soluble granules, water-in-oil emulsions, microparticles, wettable powders, water-dispersible granules, and granules. It exports its products to approximately 40 countries and regions in Asia, Europe, America, and Africa. Nantong Jiangshan Agrochemical & Chemicals Co.,Ltd. was founded in 1990 and is based in Nantong, China.
