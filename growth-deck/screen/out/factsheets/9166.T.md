## 事業の説明(yfinance)

GENDA Inc., through its subsidiaries, engages in the entertainment business in Japan, the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, the Netherlands, France, Mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, Malaysia, and Vietnam. The company operates through the Entertainment Platform and Entertainment Content segments. It operates amusement arcades under GIGO brand; nighttime entertainment business under GIGO EDGE; equipment maintenance business under GIGO SOLUTIONS; GENDA, sales and lease amusement machines; and ENTERRIUM LLC develop and operates of entertainment complexes. It also provides VR contents under Dynamo Amusement; planning and wholesale of prizes for amusement arcades under AREA Company; film production and distribution under GAGA; planning, designing and producing for entertainment-related exhibitions and events under D-EIGHT; movie information website; planning, production and sales of popcorn, sweets, and lemonade under SWEET PIXELS brand; and manufacture, and sales and import/export of jewelry water under Fillico brand. In addition, the company offers prize planning and manufacturing, character lottery operation, and e-commerce operation; planning, manufacturing and sales of character merchandise; karaoke facility management under BanBan brand; distribution of karaoke equipment under ENNE brand; import and wholesale of alcoholic beverages; foreign currency exchange machine business; and photography studio business under CARATT brand; Further, it engages in tourism; character merchandising; food and beverage; and content and promotion, including movies and interactive content businesses. The company was founded in 2018 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
