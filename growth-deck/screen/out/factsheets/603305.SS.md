## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Ningbo Xusheng Group Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, development, production, and sale of precision aluminum alloy parts in China and internationally. The company offers new energy vehicle solutions comprising electric drive systems, such as DHT dual motor drive electronic control assemblies, electric drive housing assemblies, and EV pure electric drive assembly housings; electronic control systems, consisting of EV electric control boxes, inverter housings, and inverter assembly housings; battery systems, such as PHEV battery box assemblies, battery box lower tray assemblies, and battery module frames; suspension systems, including control arms for multilink suspension systems and knuckles; body systems, such as anti-collision beam assemblies, new energy electric vehicle die-cast one-piece molding door frames, and threshold beam assemblies; and thermal management systems, which include thermal management units. It also provides energy storage systems, such as heat sinks. In addition, the company offers intelligent machinery products comprising industrial motor housings, such as servo motor housings and motor casings; and industrial valves, including washer valve bodies and high-pressure pneumatic valve housings. Further, it engages in trading, logistics, import and export trade processing, equipment leasing, and technical and sales activities. The company exports its products. The company was formerly known as Ningbo Xusheng Machinery Co., Ltd. Ningbo Xusheng Group Co., Ltd. was founded in 2003 and is headquartered in Ningbo, China.
