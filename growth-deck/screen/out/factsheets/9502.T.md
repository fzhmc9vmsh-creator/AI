## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Chubu Electric Power Company, Incorporated, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the generation, transmission, distribution, and retail of electricity in Japan and internationally. The company operates through three segments: JERA, Power Grid, and Miraiz. It generates electricity through geothermal, hydro, wind, biomass, and solar sources. The company is also involved in the fuel upstream, power generation procurement, and wholesale of electricity and gas businesses; and provides power network services. In addition, it engages in construction and manufacture related to the energy business. Further, the company is involved in the gas supply and real estate activities. Additionally, the company engages in renewable energy, nuclear power, and community support infrastructure related business, as well as overseas business. Chubu Electric Power Company, Incorporated was founded in 1889 and is headquartered in Nagoya, Japan.
