## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Guizhou Aviation Technical Development Co., Ltd researches, develops, manufactures, and sells ring forgings in China and internationally. The company offers aviation, aerospace, gas turbine, other high-end equipment, and other forgings. It also offers technology promotion and application services, sales of metal materials and metal products, and investment management activities. Its products used in aerospace, gas turbine, and high-end equipment industries, involving both cold and hot-end parts. Guizhou Aviation Technical Development Co., Ltd was founded in 2006 and is based in Guiyang, China.
