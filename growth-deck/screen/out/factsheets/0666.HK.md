## 事業の説明(yfinance)

REPT BATTERO Energy Co., Ltd. engages in the design, research and development, production, and sale of electric vehicle and energy storage system lithium-ion batteries in China and internationally. The company offers battery cell products, such as utility and C&I ESS series, residential ESS series, BEV series, PHEV and REEV series, and commercial vehicle series, as well as LFP battery cells, ternary battery cells, and EV battery modules; and battery pack products, including energy storage battery packs, passenger vehicle battery packs, and commercial vehicle battery pack and rack. It also provides ESS battery cells, modules, standardized battery boxes, battery racks, lithium-ion battery tanks for electrical energy storage, and lithium-ion battery outdoor cabinets for industrial and commercial energy storage; battery storage systems; Ultra Brain BMS, a self-developed and all-scenario battery management system; and battery recycling solutions. The company was formerly known as REPT Energy Co., Ltd. and changed its name to REPT BATTERO Energy Co., Ltd. in April 2022. REPT BATTERO Energy Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2017 and is headquartered in Wenzhou, China.
