## 事業の説明(yfinance)

TUHU Car Inc., together with its subsidiaries, operates as an integrated online and offline platform for automotive services in China. The company offers tires and chassis parts; auto maintenance services, including fluid chemicals, storage batteries and maintenance accessories, and offline maintenance; and auto repair, car detailing, and automated car wash machines and products, as well as auto accessories. It also provides automotive products and services to consumers through its online interfaces, including TUHU Automotive Service app, its website, and Weixin mini program, as well as offline stores. In addition, the company offers advertising, management of store network, warehouse and supply chain, franchises, and other services, such as insurance brokerage and agency services and software-as-a-service solutions; and sale of used cars, as well as provides used car transaction services. TUHU Car Inc. was founded in 2011 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
