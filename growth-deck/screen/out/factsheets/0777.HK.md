## 事業の説明(yfinance)

NetDragon Websoft Holdings Limited provides online and mobile games the People's Republic of China, the United States, Germany, the United Kingdom, Spain, France, and internationally. It operates through the Gaming and Application Services, Mynd.ai Business, and Property Development segments. The company's games for kids include JumpStart, School of Dragons, Neopets, World of Madagascar, and Math Blaster; and other games comprise Eudemons Online, Conquer Online, Way of the Five, Conquer Online, and Heroes Evolved. It also licenses and services developed games; sells education hardware and software products, as well as engages in the products and marketing business, mobile solutions business, and operation and development of online education business. The company was founded in 1999 and is headquartered in Fuzhou, China.
