## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Ningbo Dechang Electrical Machinery Made Co., Ltd. produces and sells electric motors and household vacuum cleaners in China and internationally. The company offers horizontal dust bag, cyclone dust cup, and charging mode vacuum cleaner products. It also offers EPS brushless motors for automobiles. The company was founded in 2002 and is based in Yuyao, China.
