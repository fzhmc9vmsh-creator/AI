## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shanghai V-Test Semiconductor Tech. Co., Ltd. operates as an integrated circuit testing provider company in China. It offers AOI, chip probing test, final test, system level test, burn in test, in tray mark, and lead scan solutions, as well as wafer testing test solution development services. The company also engages in testing equipment rental and the sale of test consumables. Its products are used in communications, computers, automotive electronics, industrial control, consumer electronics, and other applications. The company was incorporated in 2016 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
