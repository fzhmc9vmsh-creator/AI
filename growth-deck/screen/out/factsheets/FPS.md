## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Forgent Power Solutions, Inc. engages in the design and manufacture of electrical distribution equipment used in data centers, the power grid, and energy-intensive industrial facilities. The company's products include dry type transformers, liquid filled transformers, switchgear, switchboards, panelboards, remote power panels, generator connection cabinets, tap boxes, automatic transfer switches, gear ehouses, power skids, power distribution units, and ups ehouses. It also provides maintenance, testing, repair, modernization, start-up and commissioning and aftermarket retrofit services. It serves technology, power, utility and industrial companies. The company was founded in 2023 and is based in Dayton, Minnesota.
