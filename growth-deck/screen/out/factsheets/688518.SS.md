## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shenzhen United Winners Laser Co., Ltd. engages in research and development, production, and sale of precision laser welding equipment and intelligent manufacturing solutions in China and internationally. The company offers lasers, laser welding heads, laser welding machines, robotic welding workstations, and automated laser welding systems. Its products are used in power and energy storage batteries, automobile manufacturing, consumer electronics, hardware and home appliances, optical communications, medical devices, sensors, relays, and other manufacturing sectors. The company was incorporated in 2005 and is headquartered in Shenzhen, China.
