## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Bethel Automotive Safety Systems Co., Ltd develops, manufactures, and sells automotive chassis systems and advanced driving systems in China. The company offers electronic parking brake systems, wire control brake systems, electric power steering systems, electronic mechanical braking, mechanical steering gears, mechanical steering columns, aluminum knuckles, subframes, control arms, and wheel housing products. The company was founded in 2004 and is headquartered in Wuhu, China.
