## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shanghai Forest Cabin Cosmetics Group Co., Ltd. operates as a skincare brand with a focus on the anti-wrinkle and firming skincare market in China. The company offers a wide range of premium skincare solutions under Forest Cabin brand. Its products include essence oils, face creams, toners and lotions, serums, facial masks and sunscreens for cleansing and toning to moisturizing and targeted treatments, as well as provides products for anti-wrinkle, firming, and repair. It sells its products through both online and offline channels including offline stores, as well as sales to offline retailers, enterprise customers, and distributors. Shanghai Forest Cabin Cosmetics Group Co., Ltd. was founded in 2003 and is headquartered in Putuo, China.
