## 事業の説明(yfinance)

CXMT Corporation engages in the research and development, design, manufacturing, and sales of dynamic random access memory chips in China and Internationally. Its products include DDR4, DDR5, LPDDR4X, LPDDR5/5X, DRAM wafers, DRAM module. The company's other products and services include providing technology and research and development services, wafer foundry services, and sales of related photomask products. It serves servers, mobile devices, personal computers, and smart cars. The company was founded in 2016 and is based in Hefei, China.
