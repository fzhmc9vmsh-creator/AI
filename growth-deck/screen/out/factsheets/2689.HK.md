## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Nine Dragons Paper (Holdings) Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells packaging paper, printing and writing paper, and specialty paper products and pulp in the People's Republic of China. The company offers kraft, test, white top, and coated linerboard, as well as bleached folding boxboard; corrugating medium; and coated duplex board, as well as uncoated woodfree paper and office paper. It also provides micro-crepe paper, heat-resistant insulating paper, capacitor tissue paper, electrolytic capacitor paper, high-voltage insulating paper, low-voltage insulating paper, fruit cultivating bag paper, glassine paper, hot pressing pad paper, and battery base paper; corrugated sheet, cardboard, and carton box; kraft paper bag; color printing; and laminated paper products. The company was founded in 1995 and is headquartered in Dongguan, the People's Republic of China. Nine Dragons Paper (Holdings) Limited is a subsidiary of Best Result Holdings Limited.
