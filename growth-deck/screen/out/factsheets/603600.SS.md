## 事業の説明(yfinance)

UE Furniture Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, development, manufacturing, and sale of sitting healthy systems in China and Internationally. It offers back support, gaming, lounge, and office chairs, as well as business sofas, height-adjustable desks, massage chair bodies, accessories, and other sitting products. The company is also involved in commodity circulation; and investment management. It sells its products through stores and online. The company was formerly known as Zhejiang UE Furniture Co., Ltd. and changed its name to UE Furniture Co., Ltd. in December 2015. UE Furniture Co., Ltd. was founded in 2001 and is headquartered in Huzhou, China.
