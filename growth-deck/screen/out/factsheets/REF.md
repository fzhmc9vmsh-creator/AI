## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Reformation Inc. manufactures and sells sustainable women's clothing and accessories in the United States and Internationally. It offers dresses, tops, pants, skirts, shorts, jeans, sweaters, shoes, jumpsuits, two-piece sets, outerwear, sleep and loungewear, swimwear, and accessories. The company also offers wedding collection. It retails its product through website, online channels, and stores, well as through wholesalers and other channels. The company was formerly known as REF Topco, Inc. and changed its name to Reformation Inc. in April 2025. Reformation Inc. was founded in 2009 and is headquartered in Vernon, California.
