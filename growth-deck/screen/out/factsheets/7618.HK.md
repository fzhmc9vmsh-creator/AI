## 事業の説明(yfinance)

JINGDONG Industrials, Inc. provides supply chain-based technology and services in the People's Republic of China. It engages in the provision of technology; marketing services; sale and procurement of industrial products, as well as cross-border industrial supply chain technology and services, and e-commerce marketplaces. It is also involved in the online retail of industrial products; and provides technical, advertising, and information services. The company was formerly known as JD Industrial Technology Inc. The company was founded in 2017 and is headquartered in Beijing, China. JINGDONG Industrials, Inc. operates as a subsidiary of JD Industrial Technology Limited.
