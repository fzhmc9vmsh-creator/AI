## 事業の説明(yfinance)

HOTLAND HOLDINGS Co., Ltd., through its subsidiaries, operates restaurants in Japan and internationally. The company is also involved in the development of Takoyaki shops and specialty stores, as well as bar business; staple food development business; manufacture, wholesale, and sale of frozen Takoyaki; and wholesale of seafood, as well as hosting of Japanese food events. In addition, it engages in the planning and graphic design; architecture, interior and exterior design, and sign construction; store promotion support; new business development; management of stores and restaurants; import/export/processing/sale of food and other merchandises; and development of education, restaurant, and other businesses, as well as provision of packaging services. The company was formerly known as HOTLAND Co.,Ltd. and changed its name to HOTLAND HOLDINGS Co., Ltd. in April 2025. HOTLAND HOLDINGS Co., Ltd. was founded in 1988 and is based in Tokyo, Japan.
