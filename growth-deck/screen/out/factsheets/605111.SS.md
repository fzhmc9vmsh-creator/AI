## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Wuxi NCE Power Co.,Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, design, development, and sale of semiconductor chips and power devices in China and internationally. It offers shielded gate, super junction, and trench metal-oxide semiconductor field-effect transistors (MOSFET), as well as silicon carbide (SiC) MOSFET; insulated gate bipolar transistors (IGBT); intelligent power modules (IPM); and power integrated modules (PIM). The company also provides automotive-grade power devices; power modules; gallium nitride (GaN) HEMT, gate driver and power management integrated circuits, microcontroller units (MCU), and other products; and support services. In addition, it offers power electronic components and software; integrated and electronic circuits; and technology transfer and technical services. Further, the company engages in investment activities. It's products are used in industry, home appliances, consumer electronics, new energy vehicles and charging piles, intelligent robots, AI servers and data centers, drones, industrial automation, photovoltaic energy storage, 5G communications, smart homes, security, medical equipment, lithium battery protection, and other applications. Wuxi NCE Power Co.,Ltd. was founded in 2013 and is headquartered in Wuxi, China.
