## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Suzhou SONAVOX Electronics Co.,Ltd. engages in loudspeaker manufacturing worldwide. The company offers woofer, tweeter, midrange, and subwoofer loudspeakers; amplifiers; acoustic vehicle alerting systems; and services in the design of complete sound systems. It is also involved in research, design, and manufacturing of audio component and automotive sound systems. Suzhou SONAVOX Electronics Co.,Ltd. was founded in 1975 and is headquartered in Suzhou, China.
