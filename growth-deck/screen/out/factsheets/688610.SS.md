## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Hefei I-TEK OptoElectronics Co., Ltd. designs, develops, manufactures, and markets industrial imaging, high precision optics, and opto-electrical equipment in China. It offers line-scan and area-scan cameras, frame grabbers, and other products. The company also provides imaging spectrometers and intelligent focus system. Hefei I-TEK OptoElectronics Co., Ltd. was founded in 2011 and is based in Hefei, China.
