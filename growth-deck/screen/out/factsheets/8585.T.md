## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Orient Corporation, together with its subsidiaries, provides consumer finance services in Japan. It operates through five segments: Settlement and Guarantee Business; Overseas Business; Card and Loan Business; Individual Installment Payment Business; and Bank Guarantee Business. The company offers auto loans and leasing, and shopping credit, as well as home loans. It also provides credit card shopping, cash advances, and consumer loans service; purpose, general-purpose, and card loan guarantees; and rent settlement guarantee and accounts receivable, guarantee service for small leases, and collection agent services. In addition, the company bank loan guarantee services for personal loans provided by affiliated financial institutions. The company was formerly known as Orient Finance. Orient Corporation was incorporated in 1951 and is headquartered in Chiyoda, Japan.
