## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Jiangsu Cai Qin Technology Co., Ltd engages in the research and development, production, and sale of microwave dielectric ceramic components and in China and internationally. It produces dielectric filters, resonators, duplexers, aerospace products, and multiplexers, and satellite timing antennas. The company serves mobile communications, satellite, navigation and positioning, satellite communications, aerospace, electronic devices, internet of everything, and automotive industries. Jiangsu Cai Qin Technology Co., Ltd was founded in 2004 and is based in JiangSu, China.
