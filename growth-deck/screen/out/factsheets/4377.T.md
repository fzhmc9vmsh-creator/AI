## 事業の説明(yfinance)

ONE CAREER Inc. engages in the career data platform business. The company operates ONECAREER, a media that supports new graduate recruitment, as well as combines content that gathers information on job-hunting experiences; ONE CAREER PLUS, a job-seeking website; and ONE CAREER CLOUD to support the promotion of digital transformation in recruitment activities. The company was incorporated in 2015 and is based in Tokyo, Japan.
