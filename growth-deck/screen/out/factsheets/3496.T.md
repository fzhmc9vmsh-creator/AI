## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Azoom Co.,Ltd provides various real estate services in Japan. The company offers CarParking, a monthly parking search site; CarParking; CarParking One, a monthly parking lot management system for managers; Tomemiru, a car parking management service for companies; Car parking cost reduction service to support refinancing for parking lot; Home deParking to monetize empty parking lot; Bikeru, a monthly parking search site for motorcycles; and car parking solutions for corporations and construction companies. It also involved in business development through internet; internet media development and operation; parking lot management and introduction; and buying, selling, brokering, managing, and leasing of real estate properties. In addition, the company provides smart space reservation to support unified management of rental space operations. Further, the company offers coin pass search, a coin parking search site; and coconi, a location-based crowdsourcing site. Azoom Co.,Ltd was incorporated in 2009 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
