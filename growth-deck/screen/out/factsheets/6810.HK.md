## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shanghai Sunmi Technology Co., Ltd. operates as a Business Internet of Things (BIoT) solution provider internationally. The company's solutions integrate hardware, software, and data insights to offer digital transformation for business operations, such as payments, membership management, order fulfillment, inventory control, and workforce management. Its BIoT solutions mainly include smart devices and BIoT platform as a service platform. The company's smart devices use its proprietary operating system, namely SUNMI OS. Further, its BIoT PaaS platform offers a unified software infrastructure equipped with ready-to-use development tools that enable merchants and developers to develop, manage and upgrade vertical-specific software applications for use on smart devices. The company's smart devices are built on the SUNMI CBB hardware platform technology. Its flagship product, V Series, is a smart device designed for mobile internet application scenarios. The company's cloud-based architecture offers a comprehensive remote management suite comprising hundreds of microservices, such as device status monitoring, remote control and customizable desktop configurations. Additionally, its AI-driven low-code development tools tailored for commercial use and integrated AI algorithms into smart devices. The company was formerly known as Shanghai Woyou Information Technology Co., Ltd. The company was founded in 2013 and is based in Shanghai, China.
