## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Yiwu Huading Nylon Co.,Ltd. engages in the research, development, manufacturing, and sale of nylon filaments primarily in China. The company offers pre-oriented yarn, highly oriented yarn, fully drawn yarn, air-coated yarn, air-textured yarn, and elastic yarn products. Its products are used for outdoor, sports and leisure clothing, swimwear, down jackets, underwear, and military specialty textiles. The company also exports its products to South America, Europe, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and other countries and regions. Yiwu Huading Nylon Co.,Ltd. was founded in 2002 and is based in Yiwu, China.
