## 事業の説明(yfinance)

GUILIN FUDA Co.,Ltd. researches and develops, produces, and sells auto parts and components in China. The company's products include engine crankshafts, automobile clutches, new energy electric drive gears, high-strength bolts, spiral bevel gears, precision forgings, and bolts for use in the non-road machinery and high-end machinery manufacturing fields, such as marine, nuclear power, construction machinery, and agricultural machinery. The company was founded in 1995 and is headquartered in Guilin, China. GUILIN FUDA Co.,Ltd. is a subsidiary of Guilin fuda group co., LTD.
