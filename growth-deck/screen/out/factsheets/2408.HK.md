## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Guangzhou Xiao Noodles Catering Management Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the self-operated noodle restaurant and manages franchise operations in Mainland China. It also provides catering management service. The company operates under the brand name of Xiao Noodles. It serves restaurant customers, franchisees, and businesses requiring catering and supply chain solutions primarily in the PRC (People's Republic of China). The company was formerly known as Guangzhou Meet Xiaomian Catering Management Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Guangzhou Xiao Noodles Catering Management Co., Ltd. in September 2023. Guangzhou Xiao Noodles Catering Management Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2014 and is headquartered in Guangzhou, China.
