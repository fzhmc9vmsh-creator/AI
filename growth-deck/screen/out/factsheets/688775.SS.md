## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Arashi Vision Inc. engages in the research, development, production, and sale of panoramic cameras and action cameras worldwide. The company offers Insta360, a spherical camera used to handle 360-degree panoramic pictures, videos, and live streams. Its products include Insta360 ONE X2, Insta360 ONE R, Insta360 GO, Insta360 EVO, Insta360 ONE X, Insta360 ONE, Insta360 Nano S, Insta360 Nano, Insta360 Air, Insta360 Pro 2, Insta360 Titan, and Insta360 Pro cameras. It has a strategic collaboration with Leica to develop and manufacture the ONE R 1-Inch Edition and Luna Series cameras. The company was founded in 2014 and is based in Shenzhen, China.
