## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Beijing Huafeng Test & Control Technology Co.,Ltd. engages in the research and development, production, and sale of semiconductor automated test systems and accessories in China, Taiwan, the United States, Europe, Japan, South Korea, Southeast Asia, and India. The company's products include STS8200 for use in the testing analog, mixed-signal, and power integrated circuits, such as power management, signal chains, smart power modules, and third-generation compound semiconductors (GaN); STS8300 for use in the testing power management and mixed-signal integrated circuits for pin counts, performance, and various workstations; and power module test products for high-power IGBT/SiC power modules and KGD tests. It also provides STS8600 for use in testing SoC chips, including high-speed digital circuits, high-performance mixed circuits, microwave/RF circuits, communication interface circuits, CPU chips, etc. The company serves customers in the fields of integrated circuit design, IDM, wafer manufacturing, packaging, and testing. Beijing Huafeng Test & Control Technology Co.,Ltd. was founded in 1993 and is headquartered in Beijing, China.
