## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Cofoe Medical Technology Co.,Ltd. engages in the research and development, production, sales, and service of home care medical devices in China. The company offers medical and wellness products for various types of diseases treatment, damage therapy, daily health maintenance, and life quality improvement. It also offers rehabilitation aids products, such as hearing aids, electric wheelchair, and orthopedic/posture correction products; medical care products, such as dressing products and ostomy care products; health monitoring products for monitoring human physiological parameters including metabolic disease testing product and thermometers; respiratory support products including non-invasive ventilators and nebulizers; and TCM therapy and other products, such as physiotherapy devices. Additionally, the company offers logistics and warehousing services to third party medical product companies and on-line storemanagement services. Further, the company manufactures its products through the Original Design Model, through which, it collaborates with customers to develop designs of products itmanufactures; and the original equipment manufacturing model, through which its customers provide their designs and it manufactures. The company sells its products through both offline and online channels comprising direct sales primarily via self-owned and online stores, sales to third party pharmacy chain operators, sales to offline and online distributors, and through e-commerce platforms. It sells its products through its brands Cofoe, JOYOR HearingCare, Jerry Medical, Huazhou, and babaka. The company was formerly known as Hunan Cofoe Medical Technology Development Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Cofoe Medical Technology Co.,Ltd. in December 2026. Cofoe Medical Technology Co.,Ltd. was founded in 2005 and is headquartered in Changsha, China.
