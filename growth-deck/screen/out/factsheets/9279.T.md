## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Gift Holdings Inc. operates and manages restaurants in Japan. The company manufactures and sells noodles, sauce, soup, gyoza dumplings, and pork slices,. It operates its restaurants under BUTAYAMA, GANSO ABURADO, GATTON, NAGAOKA SHOKUDO, SHI-TEN-NOH, Akamisoya, Tokyo Ramen Yokocho, E.A.K. Ramen, and Machida Shoten brand names. Gift Holdings Inc. was founded in 2008 and is headquartered in Shibuya, Japan.
