## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Asymchem Laboratories (Tianjin) Co., Ltd. provides contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) solutions in Mainland China and internationally. It is involved in small molecule CDMO services for projects covering multiple major disease treatment areas, such as oncology, antiviral, anti-infective, cardiovascular, and diabetes; chemical macromolecule CDMO; biomacromolecule CDMO, including antibody-peptide conjugates; formulation CDMO; clinical CRO; synthetic biology and new technology output business. The company also engages in key pharmaceutical intermediates, APIs, and formulations activities. The company was founded in 1998 and is headquartered in Tianjin, China.
