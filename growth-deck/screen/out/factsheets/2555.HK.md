## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Sichuan Baicha Baidao Industrial Co., Ltd., an investment holding company, provides tea drink products in the People's Republic of China. It is also involved in the supply chain management activities; franchise management, marketing campaigns, and operation activities; production of biodegradable straws and packaging; IT and maintenance services; provision of trainings franchisees; provision for logistics and distribution services; and production of raw materials. The company offers its products through self-operated and franchised stores under the ChaPanda brand name. The company was founded in 2008 and is headquartered in Chengdu, the People's Republic of China. Sichuan Baicha Baidao Industrial Co., Ltd. is a subsidiary of Sichuan Hengsheng Herui Industrial Group Co., Ltd.
