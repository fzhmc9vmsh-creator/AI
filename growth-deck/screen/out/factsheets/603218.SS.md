## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Riyue Heavy Industry Co.,Ltd. engages in the research and development, production, and sale of large-scale heavy industry equipment castings in China. The company offers wind power castings, plastic machinery castings, diesel engine castings, machining center castings and other castings. The company was founded in 2007 and is headquartered in Ningbo, China.
