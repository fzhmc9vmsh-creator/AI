## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shenghe Resources Holding Co., Ltd engages in the research and development, production, and application of rare earth and related products. The company offers rare earth products, such as rare earth concentrates, compounds, oxides, and metals. It also offers mineral sands comprising zircon sands, titanium concentrate, rutile, kyanite, garnet and monazite, etc. The company's products are used in new energy vehicles, wind power generation, energy saving motors, low carbon emission reduction, precision casting, aerospace, refectory materials, and petrochemicals. It serves new energy, new materials, energy conservation and environmental protection, aerospace, military, and electronic information industries. The company was formerly known as Taiyuan Tiancheng Science & Technology Co., Ltd and changed its name to Shenghe Resources Holding Co., Ltd in January 2013. Shenghe Resources Holding Co., Ltd was founded in 1998 and is based in Chengdu, China.
