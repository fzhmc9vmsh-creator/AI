## 事業の説明(yfinance)

North Pacific Bank,Ltd. provides various banking products and services for individuals and corporations in Japan. The company offers savings, foreign currency, and time deposits; financial product and online financial product intermediation; government and public bonds for individuals; fund wrap; and investment trusts. It also provides home, car, education, home renovation, card, and personal loans; insurance and online insurance products; inheritance and trust related products; deposits for educational donations; and defined contribution pension. In addition, the company offers debit and credit cards; air post; web account transfer reception services; bank pay; ATM service; and payment services. Further, it engages in the dispatching of general workers business. The company was formerly known as Hokuyo Sogo Bank, Ltd. and changed its name to North Pacific Bank,Ltd. in 1989. North Pacific Bank,Ltd. was incorporated in 1917 and is headquartered in Sapporo, Japan.
