## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Beijing Bayi Space LCD Technology Co.,Ltd. researches, develops, produces, and sells display materials in China and internationally. It also offers semiconductors and pharmaceutical materials. The company also provides liquid crystal, polymer dispersed liquid crystal (PDLC) smart films, and organic electroluminescent materials (OLED); semiconductor materials include photoresist resin materials and polyimide; and pharmaceutical materials, such as advanced and active pharmaceutical intermediates, as well as offers liquid crystal materials. In addition, it offers mixed liquid crystal; monomer liquid crystals; and OLED and intermediate materials. Its products are used in various applications, including mobile phones, laptops, computer monitors, and televisions. The company was founded in 2004 and is headquartered in Beijing, China.
