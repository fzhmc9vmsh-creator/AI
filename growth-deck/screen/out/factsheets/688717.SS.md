## 事業の説明(yfinance)

SolaX Power Network Technology (Zhejiang) Co., Ltd. provides photovoltaic energy storage solution in China and internationally. It offers residential, commercial and industrial energy storage inverters, batteries, all-in-one ESS, and string inverters; EV chargers; ESS and battery accessories; smart charging piles; data collection controllers; data monitoring modules; microinverters; and heat pumps. The company also provides solutions for residential, commercial and industrial, utility-scale plant, and microinverter solutions, as well as smart energy management solutions. SolaX Power Network Technology (Zhejiang) Co., Ltd. was founded in 2012 and is based in Hangzhou, China.
