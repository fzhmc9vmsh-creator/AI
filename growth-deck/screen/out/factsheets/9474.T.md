## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Zenrin Co., Ltd. engages in the collection and management of a range of geospatial information worldwide. It manufactures and sells maps, including residential maps, map databases, and other content; and map database maintenance services, as well as provides map services, map software, in-car navigation software, and related IT services. The company also manufactures and sells printed materials; development and sales of web systems; research and development of software; development of map database; development and sales of package system; provides marketing and business solutions; and location-based services. In addition, it is involved in the development of software and services related to Zenrin integrated geospatial system, and corporate venture capital business. Further, the company offers easy sales promotion map, and building point data services; residential map for web, planned maps / custom-made maps, and drone; and 3D map data, logistics services, and ScanX services, as well as engages in mobility solutions business. Zenrin Co., Ltd. was founded in 1948 and is headquartered in Kitakyushu, Japan.
