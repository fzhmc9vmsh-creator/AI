## 事業の説明(yfinance)

First Sponsor Group Limited, an investment holding company, invests in, develops, and sells residential and commercial properties in the People's Republic of China, Europe, and internationally. The company operates through Property Development, Property Investment, Property Financing, and Hotel Operations segments. It provides interest-bearing loans to associates, joint ventures, and third parties. The company also engages in the subscription of debt securities; operations and provision of property financing services; development and purchase of properties for sale; development and purchase of investment properties, including hotels for rental income; and development projects that include offices, retail, residential and hotel developments, as well as owns and operates hotels and a hotspring. The company was incorporated in 2007 and is headquartered in Singapore.
