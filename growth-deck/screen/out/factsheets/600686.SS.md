## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Xiamen King Long Motor Group Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, design, development, manufacture, and sale of passenger and commercial vehicles in China and internationally. The company offers light, medium-sized, and large buses for passenger and group transport, tourism, public transport, school buses, and special vehicles under the King Long Bus and Higer Bus brands; and new energy heavy trucks, logistics vehicles, RVs, and autonomous driving products. It also provides automotive accessories; in-vehicle intelligent communication equipment; molds; new energy three-electric systems; automobile bodies; vehicle power domain controllers; commercial vehicle power battery systems; multi-in-one integrated control systems; electric axle drive control systems; automotive chassis and related parts; automotive air conditioning; commercial vehicle battery thermal management systems and related components; and automotive seats. In addition, the company engages in equity investment; engineering and technology research and development; bus industry investment; motor vehicle inspection; import and export of goods; vocational training; bus repair; and trading activities. It exports its products. Xiamen King Long Motor Group Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1988 and is based in Xiamen, China.
