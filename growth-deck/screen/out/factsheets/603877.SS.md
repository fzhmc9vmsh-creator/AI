## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Ningbo Peacebird Fashion Co.,Ltd. operates as a customer-centric fashion brand retailer in China. The company offers women's, men's and children's clothing products under the PEACEBIRD WOMEN, PEACEBIRD MEN, Mini Peace, LEDIN, and other brand names. It also retails and wholesales clothing and accessories; manufactures feathers; and offers technical development, consulting, exchanges, transfer, and promotion services. In addition, the company is involved in clothing and apparel retailing and wholesaling; garment manufacturing; technical services, development, consulting, exchange, transfer, and promotion activities; professional design services; shoes wholesaling and retailing; hats wholesaling and retailing; cosmetics wholesaling and cosmetics retailing; feather products manufacturing; daily necessities sales; general merchandise sales; textile sales; stationery wholesale; stationery retail; sporting goods and equipment retail; cultural and educational equipment rental; daily-use masks; and offers class I medical devices. It sells its products through direct-sale and franchise stores; and e-commerce platforms Ningbo Peacebird Fashion Co.,Ltd. was founded in 1996 and is headquartered in Ningbo, China.
