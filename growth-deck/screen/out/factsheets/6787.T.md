## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Meiko Electronics Co., Ltd. engages in the design, manufacture, and sale of printed circuit boards (PCBs) and other electronic related products in Japan, China, Vietnam, the rest of Asia, the United States, Europe, and internationally. Its products portfolio includes double-sided/multi-layer, HDI, flexible, rigid-flex, high electric current, embedded devices, layer, copper inlay, millimetre wave radar, heavy copper, and through hole PCBs, as well as liquidloop and metal base heat dissipation PCBs; PCB testers, solder stencils, and imaging devices and systems; and mounting jig, metal mask, and component mounting. The company also offers circuit design and electronic manufacturing services. Its products have applications in various industries, including automotive, communication and industrial equipment, AV/home appliance, office printer, amusement, medical care, computer peripherals, and aviation and aerospace. Meiko Electronics Co., Ltd. was formerly known as Meiko Denshi Kogyo Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Meiko Electronics Co., Ltd. in April 1991. Meiko Electronics Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1975 and is based in Ayase, Japan.
