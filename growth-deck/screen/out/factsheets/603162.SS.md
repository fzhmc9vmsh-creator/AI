## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Fujian Highton Development Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the coastal and international ocean dry bulk shipping business in China and internationally. It is involved in the transportation business of ore, water slag, and other dry bulk cargoes; voyage and time charter businesses; general cargo shipping; ship brokerage; and ship management business. The company was founded in 2009 and is headquartered in Fuzhou, China.
