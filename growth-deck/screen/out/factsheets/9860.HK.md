## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Adicon Holdings Limited, an investment holding company, provides medical testing services in the People's Republic of China. The company offers testing services primarily to hospitals and health check centers through an integrated network of self-operated laboratories; and research and clinical trial services. It is also involved in the trading of medical testing equipment. The company was founded in 2004 and is headquartered in Hangzhou, the People's Republic of China.
