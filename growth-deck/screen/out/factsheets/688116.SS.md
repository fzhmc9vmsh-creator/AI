## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Jiangsu Cnano Technology Co., Ltd. researches, develops, produces, and sells carbon nanotube materials and related products in China and internationally. The company offers carbon nanotube powder, carbon nanotube conductive paste, graphene composite conductive paste, carbon nanotube conductive masterbatch, etc. Its products are used in lithium batteries, conductive polymer composites, antistatic coatings, tire rubber reinforcement, and other applications. The company was founded in 2011 and is headquartered in Zhenjiang, China.
