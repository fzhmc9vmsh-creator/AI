## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Zhongsheng Group Holdings Limited, an investment holding company, engages in the sale and service of motor vehicles in the People's Republic of China. The company operates 4S dealerships for various automobile brands consisting of luxury automobile brands, including Mercedes-Benz, Lexus, BMW, Audi, Jaguar Land Rover, Porsche, Volvo and AITO, as well as mid-to-high end automobile brands, such as Toyota, Nissan, and Honda. It also provides spare parts, automobile accessories, repair and maintenance services, and other automobile-related products and services. In addition, the company offers sales and service of automobile insurance; and provision of other services. The company was founded in 1998 and is headquartered in Dalian, the People's Republic of China.
