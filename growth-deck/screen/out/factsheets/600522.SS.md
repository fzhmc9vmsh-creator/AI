## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Jiangsu Zhongtian Technology Co., Ltd. produces and sells electrical machinery and equipment for the communications, electric power, marine, new energy, marine engineering construction, and other business sectors in China and internationally. It provides telecommunication products, including PON, optical fiber cables, Fttx, coaxial cables, antennas, telecom power systems, optical transceivers, and micro modular data center products; and submarine cables comprising medium voltage, HVAC, and HVDC submarine cables, as well as EPIC, submarine fiber optic cables, and accessories. The company also offers hardware fittings, insulators, arresters, OPGW, conductors, aluminum-clad steel wires, transformers, and power switch gears, as well as LV, MV, and HV power cables; lithium-ion batteries, solar cell back sheets, and photovoltaic technology; and new materials and components, including 3D printing, supercapacitors, open cell aluminum foam, and polyimide films. In addition, it engages in the engineering, procurement, and construction activities; and non-ferrous metal trading and other business activities. Jiangsu Zhongtian Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in 1976 and is based in Nantong, the People's Republic of China.
