## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Bloks Group Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the design, development, and sale of toy products in Mainland China, the rest of Asia, the Americas, and internationally. The company offers assembly character toys, animal-themed assembly toys, assembly vehicle toys, and brick-based toys. It sells its products through a multi-channel sales network, which consists of offline sales channels, such as distributors and consignment sales; and online sales channels, including various e-commerce platforms. Bloks Group Limited was founded in 2014 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
