## 事業の説明(yfinance)

AIMA Technology Group CO., LTD engages in the research and development, manufacturing, and sale of electric bicycles, electric mopeds, electric motorcycles, and electric tricycles in Mainland China and internationally. Its products are used in travel scenarios, such as urban, commuting, rural transportation, and leisure activities. The company was founded in 1999 and is headquartered in Tianjin, China.
