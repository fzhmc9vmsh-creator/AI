## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Lingyi iTech (Guangdong) Company, through its subsidiaries, engages in manufacturing and sales of electronic equipment, automotive and low-altitude economy products in China, and internationally. The company provides Heat dissipation and power supply products made of stainless steel, composite materials, aerospace-grade titanium, and 3D dual heat sources, and wide range of materials and forms; battery power solutions, such as travel, game, and standard adapters; CNC machining, 3D printing, powder metallurgy (MIM), and die-casting manufacturing services covering the structural components for robots; joint modules, high-power charging, heat dissipation modules, servo motors, drivers, reducers, design, and assembly services for the main body, and dexterous hands of robots. It also offers server liquid cooling quick-connect connectors (UQD), liquid cooling manifolds, single-phase liquid cooling modules (server liquid cooling plates - liquid cooling plate and optical module cold plates), phase change liquid cooling modules, server heat sinks (VC and 3DVC), as well as busbars and server racks; automotive transmission systems, interior and exterior components, and core components of new energy batteries; and carbon fiber aircraft rotating propeller blades and high-power charging systems, etc. The company also involved in the development, production, sales and related services of the hardware for AI mobile phones and foldable screen phones, AI PCs and tablets, imaging display, materials, battery power supply, thermal management (heat dissipation), AI glasses and XR wearable devices, high-end assembly and other, sensors, and related modules, robots, etc. The company was founded in 2006 and is headquartered in Jiangmen, China. Lingyi iTech (Guangdong) Company is a subsidiary of Lingsheng Investment (Jiangsu) Co., Ltd.
