## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Zhuhai CosMX Battery Co., Ltd. manufactures and supplies polymer lithium-ion batteries worldwide. The company offers energy storage for ESS cells, residential, telecom, and MAX Green series; and power battery for power cells, automotive, two-wheeler, and LAV batteries. It serves PCs, notebooks, tablets, smart phones, smart wearables, power tools, drones, and other fields. The company was formerly known as Zhuhai Coslight Battery Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Zhuhai CosMX Battery Co., Ltd. in March 2019. Zhuhai CosMX Battery Co., Ltd. was founded in 2007 and is headquartered in Zhuhai, China.
