## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Legend Holdings Corporation, along with its subsidiaries, operates in the industrial operations and industrial incubations and investments sectors in the People's Republic of China and internationally. The company provides intelligent devices and infrastructure, creates intelligent solutions, services, and software; research, develops, produces, and sells advanced material products; engages in agriculture and food-related businesses; and offers integrated banking services, including corporate and institutional banking, retail banking, private banking, and capital markets. It also invests in private equity and venture capital funds as a limited partner; holds interest in the general partners of various funds; and makes angel investments in technology start-ups, as well as minority investments in other entities. In addition, it engages in aviation logistics, financial services, medical and healthcare, and office leasing-related businesses. Further, it develops car management software, chemicals, and energy materials; provides car services, information consulting, cold chain, insurance, investment management, and medical consultation services; and engages in real estate investment, asset management, enterprise management, and project investment. Additionally, the company is involved in agriculture product planting and trading, agricultural investment, logistics, and food investment activities; trades in, processes, and sells seafood and other animal protein-related products; offers office building rental and services; develops and manufactures new energy technologies; provides financial services; engages in banking and insurance; and produces and sells salmon. Legend Holdings Corporation was incorporated in 1984 and is headquartered in Beijing, the People's Republic of China.
