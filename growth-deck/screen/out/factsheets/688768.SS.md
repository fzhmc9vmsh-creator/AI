## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Anhui Ronds Science & Technology Incorporated Company provides solutions for machinery condition monitoring in the predictive maintenance field in China. Its main products are industrial equipment condition monitoring and fault diagnosis systems. The company offers hardware products, such as sensors, acquisition equipment, and auxiliary equipment; software covering supercare equipment intelligent operation and maintenance platform and iEAM intelligent equipment management system; and a Rongzhi Zhiwei app for the enterprise's daily maintenance and management needs for industrial equipment. It also provides software research and development, intelligent diagnosis, cloud services, etc. In addition, the company serves wind power, petrochemical, metallurgical, cement, mining, thermal power, paper making, water treatment plant, rail transit, and coal chemical industries. Further, it exports its products to Europe, South America, North America, Southeast Asia, and other regions. Anhui Ronds Science & Technology Incorporated Company was founded in 2007 and is based in Hefei, China.
