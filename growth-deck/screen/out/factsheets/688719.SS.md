## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Xi'an Actionpower Electric Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research and development, production, and sale of power electronic conversion and control equipment in China and internationally. The company offers precision test power supply products, such as universal and special AC and DC test power supply products; special power supply products, including rail transit, aircraft ground, and custom power supply products; and power quality control equipment comprising general electric energy quality control equipment, and distributing and customizing power quality control equipment. It also provides High-end industrial power supplies that includes plasma power supply, fusion power, and programmable power supply, as well as involved in technology development activities. In addition, the company offers solutions for infrastructure and advanced manufacturing, scientific research experiments, smart grids, new energy and energy vehicles, and aerospace. The company was formerly known as Xi'an Action Electronic Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Xi'an Actionpower Electric Co., Ltd. in April 2012. Xi'an Actionpower Electric Co., Ltd. was founded in 1996 and is based in Xi'an, China.
