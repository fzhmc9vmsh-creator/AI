## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Hangzhou Youngsun Intelligent Equipment Co., Ltd. engages in research and development, production and sales of packaging equipment and supporting packaging materials internationally. The company offers vacuum packaging machines, labeling machines, shrink wrappers, carton sealers, box sealing machines, blister packaging machines, strapping machines, battery strapping tools, strapping tools, palletizers, wrapping machines, depalletizing, carton packers, packing lines, PP/PET straps, stretch films/shrink films, conveyors, cartoning machines, robots, vertical packaging machines, tea/coffee bag VFFS machines, filling and capping machines, can filler seamer machines, full automatic can unloaders, pasteurizers, pre-mixers, blow molding machines, and bottle washers. It also provides case packers and sealers, case erectors, and belt/roller conveyors, as well as customized packing production lines. In addition, the company offers various software systems, such as intelligent equipment human-computer interaction, production line control, AI visual inspection, production process control, production execution management, and product traceability systems. Hangzhou Youngsun Intelligent Equipment Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2002 and is based in Hangzhou, China.
