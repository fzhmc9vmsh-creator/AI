## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Hebei Huatong Wires and Cables Group Co., Ltd. engages in the research and development, production, and sale of wires and cables in China and internationally. It operates through Cable Sector, Oil and Gas Sector, and Aluminum Sector segments. The company offers ESP cables, mining cables, rubber cables, low voltage power cables, medium voltage power cables, electrical equipment cables, as well as new energy and other cables; and umbilical tubes, intelligent coiled tubing, intelligent flat pack, ESPCP, and specialized oil and gas service vehicles. It also provides primary aluminium ingots, billets, wire rods, and aluminium scrap recycling. In addition, the company is involved in technological research and development and customized solutions, offshore survey data acquisition, and professional equipment leasing and maintenance services. Hebei Huatong Wires and Cables Group Co., Ltd. was founded in 1993 and is headquartered in Tangshan, China.
