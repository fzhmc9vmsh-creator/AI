## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Wellhope Foods Co., Ltd. produces and sells feed products for swine, poultry, ruminant animal, and other animals in China and internationally. The company is involved in broiler breeding, hatching, feed production, commercial broiler breeding, broiler slaughtering and processing, and deep processing of prepared and cooked foods. It also engages in breeding and sales of breeding pigs, sales of commercial piglets, free-range fattening pig farming and a small amount of self-raising, and pig slaughtering; fish meal, soybean meal, and co-products of corn; and amino acid, antioxidant, mold inhibitors, and vitamins, and trades products, such as vaccines and veterinary drugs. In addition, the company operates pet clinics; and manufactures biotechnology and smart farming equipment, including feed additives, biological products, animal health solutions for swine, poultry, ruminants, and other livestock, as well as farming facilities. Further, it produces prepared meat, meat balls, fried food, raw meat, semi-finished meat, and eggs. Wellhope Foods Co., Ltd. was founded in 1995 and is headquartered in Shenyang, China.
