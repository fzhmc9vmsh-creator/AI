## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shanghai Baosight Software Co.,Ltd. provides industrial solutions in China. The company offers information solutions, including manufacturing execution, integrated process quality management, and energy management systems. It provides smart storage systems, such as crane location tracking and unmanned automatic crane systems; and electromechanical equipment comprising industrial robots, bell-type furnaces, and surface inspection devices. In addition, the company provides automation solutions consisting of whole metallurgical and cold rolling processes. Shanghai Baosight Software Co.,Ltd. was founded in 1978 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
