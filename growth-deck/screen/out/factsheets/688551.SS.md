## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Hefei Kewell Power System Co.,Ltd. provides test systems and intelligent manufacturing equipment in China and internationally. It offers test power supply products, including DC power supply/DC load, AC power supply/AC load, motor simulators, and charge and discharge test systems; power semiconductor testing and intelligent manufacturing equipment comprising performance and reliability testing systems, production line automation equipment, and process equipment; and hydrogen energy testing and intelligent manufacturing equipment, such as solid oxide, electrolytic cell, fuel cell, and electrochemical testing systems, as well as the TestWell hydrogen energy testing software platform. The company also provides AI server power system testing, photovoltaic energy storage testing, new energy vehicle testing, fuel cell testing system, electrolytic cell testing system, power semiconductor test, and semiconductor packaging process equipment solutions, as well as technical, quality, and customer support services. It exports its products to Europe, Japan, South Korea, and other Southeast Asian countries and regions. The company was incorporated in 2011 and is headquartered in Hefei, China.
