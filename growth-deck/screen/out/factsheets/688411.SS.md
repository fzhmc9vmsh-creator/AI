## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Beijing HyperStrong Technology Co., Ltd. engages in the design, development, integration, and operation of energy storage power stations in China ad internationally. It offers liquid-cooling energy storage systems (ESS) under the HyperBlock M, HyperBlock III, and HyperBlock II names; HyperCubePro, a commercial battery storage system for charging stations; HyperCubeC&I, a liquid-cooling outdoor cabinet; HyperCube-Mini C, a base station distributed ESS; HyperBox, a residential energy storage system; and HyperStrong AI Platform, a full process management platform. The company provides utility-scale, commercial and industrial, and residential solutions. It also provides solutions for customers in the source-grid-load chain, including traditional power generation, new energy power generation, smart grid, and terminal power users. Further, it is involved in new energy vehicle leasing. The company was incorporated in 2011 and is based in Beijing, China.
