## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Hangzhou SF Intra-city Industrial Co., Ltd., an investment holding company, provides intra-city on-demand delivery services in the People's Republic of China. It covers the delivery of food, fresh produce, flowers, cakes and desserts, other groceries, apparel, jewelry, cosmetics, 3C electronics, as well as personal errands services, and task-based government and enterprise services, etc. The company offers software development and information technology services; procures equipment; software as a service (SaaS) solutions; and intracity delivery services. It serves intra-city delivery for merchants, and consumers, and last-mile delivery primarily for logistics companies. The company was founded in 2016 and is headquartered in Shenzhen, China. Hangzhou SF Intra-city Industrial Co., Ltd. operates as a subsidiary of Shenzhen S.F. Taisen Holding (Group) Co.,Ltd.
