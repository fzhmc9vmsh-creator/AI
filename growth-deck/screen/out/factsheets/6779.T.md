## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Nihon Dempa Kogyo Co., Ltd. engages in the manufacture and sale of crystal-related products in Japan, China, the United States, Germany, South Korea, Mexico, Romania, and internationally. The company also offers crystal units, crystal oscillators, frequency synthesizers, signal generators, millimeter-wave converters, synthetic quartz crystals, optical components, QCM outgas analysis system and real-time process monitor, outgas analysis systems, ultrasonic transducers, and SAW devices. ts products are used in various applications, space, infrastructure/industrial equipment, automotive, mobile, AV/OA, medical, healthcare, household appliance, and connectivity sectors. The company was formerly known as Nanbu Shoko Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Nihon Dempa Kogyo Co., Ltd. in 1950. Nihon Dempa Kogyo Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1948 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
