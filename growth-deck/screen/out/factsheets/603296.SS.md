## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Huaqin Co., Ltd. engages in the research, design, manufacturing, and operation services of intelligent products in China and internationally. The company offers personal electronics, including smart phones, tablets, and laptops; smart wearables; VR and AR, smart speaker, smart door lock, and TV; automotive electronics comprising intelligent cabin, connection, driving, and vehicle control; commercial devices which include POS, mobile data collection terminals, intelligent door locks, intelligent speakers, smart office, industrial webcams, and edge computing; and general purpose servers, network switches, heterogeneous servers, and edge servers, as well as digital scenes. It caters to global technology companies and the consumer electronics industry. The company sells its products through direct sales force, distributors, and global brand customers. Huaqin Co., Ltd. was formerly known as Huaqin Technology Co.ltd. and changed its name to Huaqin Co., Ltd. in December 2020. The company was founded in 2005 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
