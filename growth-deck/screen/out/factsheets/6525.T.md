## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Kokusai Electric Corporation engages in the development, manufacture, sale, repair, and maintenance of semiconductor manufacturing equipment worldwide. The company provides deposition process equipment, treatment process equipment, measuring systems, and ultrasonic generators. It also sells related parts; and new and used equipment for wafers. The company was founded in 1949 and is headquartered in Chiyoda, Japan.
