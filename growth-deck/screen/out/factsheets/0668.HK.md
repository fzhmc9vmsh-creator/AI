## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Anker Innovations Technology Co., Ltd. engages in design, manufactures, and sale of consumer electronics. It offers power banks, chargers, hubs and docking stations, cables, AC power, car chargers, balcony photovoltaic energy storage systems, residential energy storage system, security cameras, video doorbells, alarm systems, 3D-texture UV printers, wireless headphones, speakers, projectors, and conference systems. The company offers its products through online and offline retail channels. It operates its operations in the PRC, North America, Europe, and other regions. The company was incorporated in 2011 and is based in Changsha, China.
