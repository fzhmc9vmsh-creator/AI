## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Eyebright Medical Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd. engages in development, production, and commercialization of ophthalmic medical devices in China and internationally. The company provides intraocular lens, trimmers, capsular tension ring, defocus lense, orthokeratology, and contact lenses in the areas of surgical treatment, myopia management, and consumer vision care. It also offers solutions for ophthalmic surgery, refractive error correction, and optometry consumption. The company was founded in 2010 and is headquartered in Beijing, China.
