## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Eastroc Beverage (Group) Co., Ltd. engages in the manufacturing and distribution of beverages in the People's Republic of China. The company offers energy, sports, tea, coffee, plant-based protein, electrolytes, and fruit and vegetable juice beverages. It also provides eastroc beverages, including eastroc ewater, eastroc coffee master, eastroc tea, eastroc milk tea, eastroc roasted good tea, and eastroc coco island. In addition, the company engages in international trade business, as well as provision of supply chain management services. It primarily exports its products to the United States, South Korea, Malaysia, Vietnam, and Indonesia. The company was formerly known as Shenzhen Eastroc Beverage Industry Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Eastroc Beverage (Group) Co., Ltd. in March 2018. The company was incorporated in 1994 and is based in Shenzhen, China.
