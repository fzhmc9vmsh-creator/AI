## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Anhui XDLK Microsystem Corporation Limited engages in the research and development, testing, and sale of sensors in China. The company offers MEMS accelerometers and gyroscopes. Its products are used in industrial production, industrial equipment monitoring and maintenance, automotive driver assistance, meteorological monitoring, oil exploration, surveying and mapping, bridge monitoring, geological exploration, disaster early warning, and other fields. Anhui XDLK Microsystem Corporation Limited was founded in 2012 and is based in Haidian, China.
