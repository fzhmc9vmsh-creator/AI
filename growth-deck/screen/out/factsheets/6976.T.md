## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Taiyo Yuden Co., Ltd. develops, manufactures, and sells electronic components in Japan, North America, China, Europe, Hong Kong, Taiwan and internationally. The company provides products, such as multilayer ceramic capacitors, inductors, high frequency devices, RF devices, and aluminum electrolytic capacitors, as well as conductive polymer hybrid aluminum electrolytic capacitors. Taiyo Yuden Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1950 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
