## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Nishi-Nippon Financial Holdings, Inc., through its subsidiaries, engages in financial services, primarily banking operations in Japan. The company offers deposit and lending services, as well as foreign exchange and negotiable instruments. It also provides securities investment services, over-the-counter sales of investment trusts and insurance products, and other related services. The company was formerly known as The Nishi-Nippon City Bank, Ltd. and changed its name to Nishi-Nippon Financial Holdings, Inc. in October 2016. Nishi-Nippon Financial Holdings, Inc. was founded in 1912 and is headquartered in Fukuoka City, Japan.
