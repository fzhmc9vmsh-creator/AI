## 事業の説明(yfinance)

JL Mag Rare-Earth Co., Ltd. engages in the research and development, production, and sale of rare earth permanent magnetic materials in Mainland China and internationally. The company offers NdFeB PMs for use in new energy vehicles and auto parts, energy-saving variable-frequency air-conditioners, wind power generation, 3C, robotics and industrial servo motors, energy-saving elevators, rail transit, and other fields. JL Mag Rare-Earth Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2008 and is headquartered in Ganzhou, China.
