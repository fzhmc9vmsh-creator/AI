## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Jinan Shengquan Group Share Holding Co., Ltd. engages in the research, development, production, and sale of synthetic resins and composite materials, biomass chemical materials, and related products in China and internationally. The company offers biorefinery products, such as biomass methanol, precursors, hard carbon, cellulose, hemicellulose, and lignin; new energy industry products, including LTO cells, modules, forklift power supply, and outdoor portable power supply; and electronic chemicals comprising electronic chemicals and electrical materials products. It also provides phenolic and composites consisting of phenolic resins, phenolic resin for carbon materials, and transportation and construction composites; foundry materials, which include binder, coating, filter, riser, auxiliary and melting materials, equipment, and Anycasting simulation analysis software; and nucleosides, heterocycle compound, and carbohydrates, as well as L-arabinose products and health sugar. Jinan Shengquan Group Share Holding Co., Ltd. was founded in 1979 and is headquartered in Jinan, China.
