## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Critical Holdings Berhad, an investment holding company, provides MEP design and engineering solutions for data centers, cleanrooms, and plantrooms in Malaysia and Singapore. It operates through two segments: MEP Engineering Solutions and MEP Maintenance and Services. The company is involved in the design, build, project management, supply, installation, testing, and commissioning of MEP systems for newly constructed critical facilities; and redevelopment, refurbishment, and upgrading of existing facilities. It also provides maintenance support, including preventive and scheduled maintenance, as well as emergency repair services. In addition, the company offers maintenance and services support of mechanical, electrical and process utilities systems and equipment for critical facilities. Further, it serves semiconductor manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, solar photovoltaic production, telecommunications, and healthcare sectors. Critical Holdings Berhad was founded in 2013 and is headquartered in Bayan Lepas, Malaysia.
