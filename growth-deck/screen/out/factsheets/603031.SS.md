## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Anhui Anfu Battery Technology Co.,Ltd, together with its subsidiaries, researches, develops, produces, and sells batteries in China and internationally. It provides consumer, commercial small, alkaline, carbon, and other batteries, as well as digital accessories and consumer products. The company is also involved in capital market services; electrical machinery and equipment manufacturing; wholesale and retail trade; and technology promotion and application services. It exports its products. The company was formerly known as Anhui Andeli Department Store Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Anhui Anfu Battery Technology Co.,Ltd in May 2022. Anhui Anfu Battery Technology Co.,Ltd was founded in 1984 and is based in Hefei, China.
