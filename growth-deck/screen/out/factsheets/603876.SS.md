## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Jiangsu Dingsheng New Materials Joint-Stock Co.,Ltd engages in research and development, production, and sale of aluminum plates, strip, and foils. Its products portfolio includes aluminum and aluminum alloy plates, belts, sheets, and coating materials, as well as processed products comprising aluminum oxide plates, cable foils, air-conditioner foils, decorative building material aluminum foils, air conditioner refrigerator evaporators, condensers, container foils, single and double zero foils, aluminium matrix composite, battery foil, and others. The company also offers PS version aluminium strips for computers, container foils, single zero foils, double zero foils, aluminum matrix composite products, battery foils, air conditioning foils, and aluminum sheets and strips. It serves green packaging, household appliances, lithium batteries, transportation, architectural decoration, automotive, and other fields. The company was formerly known as Zhenjiang Dingsheng Aluminum Industry Co., Ltd. Jiangsu Dingsheng New Materials Joint-Stock Co.,Ltd was founded in 2003 and is based in Zhenjiang, China.
