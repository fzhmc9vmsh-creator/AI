## 事業の説明(yfinance)

AEON Fantasy Co.,LTD. operates family-oriented amusement facilities in Japan, Malaysia, Thailand, the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, and China. It also operates playgrounds and shopping malls. The company was formerly known as Jusco Co., Ltd. The company was incorporated in 1969 and is based in Chiba, Japan. AEON Fantasy Co.,LTD. operates as a subsidiary of Aeon Co., Ltd.
