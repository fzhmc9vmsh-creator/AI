## 事業の説明(yfinance)

The Shiga Bank, Ltd. provides various banking products and services primarily in Japan. The company offers various accounts, such as savings, pension term, time, property, ordinary, retirement, green and foreign currency deposits; and mortgage, card, purpose-specific, free, affiliated, auto, loan simulation, housing, and other loans. It also provides investment trust and insurance products; government bonds; asset management products; cards; asset management support/retirement allowance support plans; online financial instruments brokerage services; and web transfer, internet, and other banking services. In addition, the company offers iDeCo, shigagin big advance, IT business support and business matching, medical business start-up and management, business succession/asset succession, and M&A advisory services. The Shiga Bank, Ltd. was incorporated in 1933 and is headquartered in Otsu, Japan.
