## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Net Protections Holdings, Inc. provides buy now pay later (BNPL) payment services in Japan and internationally. The company provides NP Atobarai, a BtoC e-commerce Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) service; NP Kakebarai, a BtoB BNPL service; atone, a BtoC cardless BNPL service; AFTEE, a BtoC BNPL service in Taiwan; and NP Point Club, a point reward program. The company was founded in 2000 and is based in Tokyo, Japan.
