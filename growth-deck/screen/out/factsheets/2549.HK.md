## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Carote Ltd, an investment holding company, engages in the manufacture and sale of kitchenware products in Mainland China, the United States, Western Europe, Japan, Southeast Asia, and internationally. It offers cookware products, such as non-stick pots and pans, cast iron pots, and curated cookware sets; and kitchen utensils, drinkware, and other products. The company sells its products under the CAROTE brand through third-party e-commerce platforms, as well as through wholesale distribution channels. Carote Ltd was founded in 2007 and is headquartered in Hangzhou, China. Carote Ltd is a subsidiary of Yili Investment Holdings Ltd.
