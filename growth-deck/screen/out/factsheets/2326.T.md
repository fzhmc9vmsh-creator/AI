## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Digital Arts Inc., together with its subsidiaries, develops and markets internet security software and appliances in Japan. The company offers i-FILTER, a web security product; m-FILTER, a mail security product; FinalCode, a file encryption and tracking solution; DigitalArts@Cloud, a comprehensive cloud security product that covers web browsing, email, and files; f-FILTER, a data protection and transfer solution; a-FILTER, an integrated authentication platform; and Z-FILTER, a network security product. It also provides D-SPA, a proxy appliance product; StartIN, a single sign-on ID and ID management solution; and Desk, a chat and online conference tool. The company serves the enterprise, public, and consumer sectors. Digital Arts Inc. was incorporated in 1995 and is headquartered in Chiyoda, Japan.
