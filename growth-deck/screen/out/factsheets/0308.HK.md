## 事業の説明(yfinance)

China Travel International Investment Hong Kong Limited provides travel and tourism services. It operates through four segments: Tourist Attraction and Related Operations, Travel Document and Related Operations, Hotel Operations, and Passenger Transportation Operations. The Tourist Attraction and Related Operations segment operates theme parks, natural and cultural scenic spots in Mainland China. The Travel Document and Related Operations segment engages in the provision of travel document and related services in Hong Kong and Mainland China. The Hotel Operations segment offers hotel accommodation, and food and beverage services in Hong Kong, Macau, and Mainland China. The Passenger Transportation Operations segment provides cross-border transportation services to individuals travelling between Hong Kong, Macau, and Mainland China; and engages in vehicle and vessel rental and charter operations in Hong Kong, Macau, and Mainland China. The company was incorporated in 1992 and is based in Central, Hong Kong.
