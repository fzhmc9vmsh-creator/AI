## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Hoshine Silicon Industry Co., Ltd. engages in the production and sale of silicon-based materials in China and internationally. It offers solar panels, including N-type monocrystalline high-efficiency bifacial modules; silicon metals, silicon metal powders, and fine silicon metal powders; and graphitized carbon electrodes. The company also provides silicone monomers comprising methyltrichlorosilane, dimethyldichlorosilane, trimethylchlorosilane, and methyldichlorosilane; silane intermediates that include tetramethyldisiloxane, dimethylcyclosiloxane, hexamethylcyclotrisiloxane, octamethylcyclotetrasiloxane, and decamethylcyclopentasiloxane; methyl vinyl and liquid silicone rubbers; room temperature vulcanized silicone rubbers; acetic and neutral silicon sealants; silicon fluid, such as hydroxyl terminated polydimethylsiloxane linear, high boiling silicone oil, methyl hydrogen silicone fluid, polydimethylsiloxane, and vinyl terminated silicone fluid; and precipitated and fumed silica products. Its products are used in aerospace, military, electronic communications, photovoltaic new energy, health care, automobile, and other applications. The company was incorporated in 2005 and is headquartered in Cixi, China.
