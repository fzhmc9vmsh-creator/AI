## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Longyan Zhuoyue New Energy Co., Ltd., a renewable company, engages in the research, development, production and sales of biodiesel and bio-based materials in China. It offers FAME biodiesel, HVO biodiesel, sustainable aviation fuel, industrial glycerin, biomass plasticizers, alkyd resin, biomass fatty alcohol, etc. The company also exports its products. The company was founded in 2001 and is based in Longyan, China.
