## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Juneyao Airlines Co., Ltd, together with its subsidiaries, operates an air transport business in Mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, and internationally. The company offers air passenger and cargo transportation services; travel agency services, including air ticket sales agency services; advertising design, production, and placement services; agency services; airline ground handling, flight crew training, and simulator training; general cargo warehousing; food production and sales; investment and consulting services; and technical services in the field of internet technology and travel agency services. It also engages in logistics, aircraft leasing, catering, and import and export trade activities. The company was founded in 2006 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
