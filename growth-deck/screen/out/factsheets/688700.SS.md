## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Kunshan Dongwei Technology Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, development, manufacturing, and sale of electroplating equipment in China and internationally. The company offers vertical continuous electroplating, new energy composite copper foil, horizontal wet process, hardware continuous line, gantry, and vacuum coasting equipment. It exports its products. The company was founded in 2005 and is based in Kunshan, China.
