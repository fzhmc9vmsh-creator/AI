## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Seazen Holdings Co., Ltd engages in the real estate business in China. The company is involved in the operation and management of commercial complexes; development and sales of real estate; and hotel and eldercare businesses. It also provides agency construction services. The company was formerly known as Future Land Holdings Co., Ltd. Seazen Holdings Co., Ltd was founded in 1993 and is headquartered in Shanghai, the People's Republic of China. Seazen Holdings Co., Ltd. is a subsidiary of Fuyu Development Group Limited.
