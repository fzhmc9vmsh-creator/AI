## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Hexing Electrical Co.,Ltd. engages in smart power distribution, smart water management, and digital energy-related businesses in China and internationally. The company offers smart metering, data acquisition and communication, power distribution equipment, new energy, software and platforms, water treatment, and marine propulsion products. It also provides smart energy metering, energy management systems, C and I power distribution, data centers, billing and trading platform, treatment of distribution transformer area, feder automation, medium-voltage microgrid, renewable energy station, rural distribution area energy storage, solar-storage-charging integrated energy storage, industrial park energy storage, energy storage for commercial complexes, AMI smart metering, automatic meter reading, prepaid system, Cand I metering, district metering area and leakage control, and precise flow metering for agricultural irrigation and wastewater processes; and small craft electric propulsion systems and clean water solutions. In addition, it offers smart hardware and system software and related solutions to power companies, water utilities, and industrial and commercial users and end consumers. It serves power distribution and consumption; new energy and digital energy, and smart water solutions; smart gas solutions; and marine electrification systems industries. Hexing Electrical Co.,Ltd. was founded in 2001 and is headquartered in Hangzhou, China.
