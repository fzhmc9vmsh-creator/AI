## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Tongguan Gold Group Limited, an investment holding company, engages in gold mining operations in China. It is involved in sale of mine-produced gold, including gold concentrate, gold bullion, and related products; gold exploration, mining, processing, and smelting operations; gold recycling; and refining and sale of gold bullion. The company was formerly known as China Mining Resources Group Limited and changed its name to Tongguan Gold Group Limited in April 2018. Tongguan Gold Group Limited is headquartered in Wan Chai, Hong Kong.
