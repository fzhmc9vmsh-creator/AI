## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Unicomp Technology Group Co., Ltd. engages in the research, development, manufacture, and sale of X-ray technology and intelligent detection equipment in China. It offers electronics, semicon, lithium battery, industrial NDT, food beverage, chip counter, electrical x-ray, industrial CT, and x-ray source products. Its products has applications in electronics, semicon, battery, foundries, automotive, welding, military, science and research industries. Unicomp Technology Group Co., Ltd. was formerly known as Wuxi Unicomp Technology Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Unicomp Technology Group Co., Ltd. in June 2025. The company was founded in 2002 and is based in Shenzhen, China.
