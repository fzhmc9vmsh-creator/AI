## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shenzhen Investment Limited, an investment holding company, develops, invests, and manages real estate properties in Mainland China. It operates through Property Development; Property Investment; Property Management; Manufacture; and Others segments. The company invests in and develops residential and commercial properties; invests in commercial properties; manages properties; and manufactures and sells industrial products. It is also involved in hotels and warehouses operations; sale of agricultural products; and provision of construction services and other businesses. The company was incorporated in 1992 and is based in Tsim Sha Tsui, Hong Kong. Shenzhen Investment Limited operates as a subsidiary of Shum Yip Holdings Company Limited.
