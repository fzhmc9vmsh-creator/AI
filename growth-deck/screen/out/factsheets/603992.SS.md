## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Xiamen Solex High-tech Industries Co., Ltd. designs, manufactures, and sells kitchen and bathroom, beauty, and health software and hardware products in China and internationally. It offers system integration solutions; smart kitchen and bathroom products, and other products; and offers oral health, sports rehabilitation and shaping, healthy beverages, and companionship, encompassing AI-powered smart mattresses, anti-snoring pillows, sleep monitors, hair removal devices, hair growth devices, oral irrigators, electric toothbrushes, and smart yoga mats products. The company also offers showers, faucets, smart toilets; and beauty instruments and smart skin testers, as well as soda dispensers and intimate care devices. The company was founded in 2004 and is based in Xiamen, China.
