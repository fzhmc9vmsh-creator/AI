## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shiyue Daotian Group Co., Ltd. manufactures and sells pantry staple food in the People's Republic of China. The company operates through four segments: Rice Products; Corn Products; Whole Grain, Bean, and Other Products; and Dried Food and Other Products. The Rice Products segment produces pre-packaged rice products of different varieties, such as wuchang rice, daoxiang rice, long grain rice, and komachi rice. The Corn Products segment produces pre-packaged corn products, such as yellow, white, black, and multicolored glutinous corn, fruit corn, fruit corn kernels, flavored corn, as well as dried and peeled corn kernels. The Whole Grain, Bean, and Other Products segment provides pre-packaged mixed brown rice, millet, red bean, sesame, and corn and seed products. The Dried Food and Other Products segment offer pre-packaged wood ear mushrooms, snow fungus mushrooms, lotus seeds, and lily bulbs as well as by-products, such as bran, husk, and fractioned rice. The company is also involved in the investment activities and provision of technology services. In addition, it sells its products under the Shiyue Daotian, Firewood Courtyard, and Sunshine Family brand names through e-commerce platform, online self-operated stores, modern retail channels, distribution network, and direct to customers. The company was formerly known as Shiyue Daotian Agricultural Technology Co., Ltd. Shiyue Daotian Group Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2018 and is headquartered in Beijing, the People's Republic of China.
