## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Musashi Seimitsu Industry Co., Ltd. manufactures and sells automotive parts in Japan, the United States, Asia, China, and Europe. The company's power train business offers differential mechanism products comprising differential assembly and gear; decelerating mechanisms, including planetary assemblies; engine intake and exhaust mechanisms, such as cast and assembled camshafts, and balance shafts; and shift gear mechanisms comprising transmission gears. Its linkage and suspension business provides connecting, absorbing, and steering transmission mechanisms comprising suspension arm assemblies, and suspension and steering ball joints. The company's motorcycle business offers power transmitting mechanisms, such as cover assemblies, dual clutch transmissions, transmission assemblies, reduction gears for scooters, and other gears for motorcycles; and PF camshafts, assembled camshafts, and camshafts and bevel gears for small, medium-sized, and large motorcycles, as well as general-purpose engines, and outboard motors. In addition, the company offers manufacturing and sales of AI visual inspection machines; and prototyping and mass production contract manufacturing services. The company was formerly known as Musashi Sangyo and changed its name to Musashi Seimitsu Industry Co., Ltd. in September 1963. Musashi Seimitsu Industry Co., Ltd. was founded in 1938 and is headquartered in Toyohashi, Japan.
