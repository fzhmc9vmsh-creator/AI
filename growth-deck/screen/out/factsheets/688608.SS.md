## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Bestechnic (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. engages in the research, design, and sale of low-power wireless computing SoC chips in China and internationally. The company offers wireless audio chips, smart wearable chips, and smart hardware chips. Its products are used in low-power smart audio and video terminals for smart wearables and smart hardware, including Bluetooth headsets, smartwatches, and smart glasses. The company was founded in 2015 and is based in Shanghai, China.
