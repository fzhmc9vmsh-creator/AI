## 事業の説明(yfinance)

XIANGPIAOPIAO Food Co.,Ltd, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, development, production, and sale of milk tea products in China and internationally. The company offers liquid milk tea, instant milk tea, cup-packaged fruit tea, Hong Kong-style tea, and ready-to-drink milk tea under the Xiang Piao Piao Senpure, Meco, and LAN Fong Yuen brands. It also provides technology promotion and application; supply chain management and transportation agency; and business services. The company sells its products through dealers and distributors, as well as through its snack retail stores. It exports its products. XIANGPIAOPIAO Food Co.,Ltd was founded in 2005 and is based in Hangzhou, China.
