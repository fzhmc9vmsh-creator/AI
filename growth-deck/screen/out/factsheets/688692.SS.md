## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Wuhan Dameng Database Company Limited provides database product development services in China. It offers DM8, a database management system; DMDSC, a data sharing cluster; DMMPP, a large-scale parallel processing cluster; DMDataWatch, a data watch cluster; DMRWC, a read/write separation cluster; DMCDM, a cache database; and DAMENG, a graph database management system. The company also provides technical services; cloud computing and big data solutions; and industry software solutions. It serves the energy, communications, transport, education, and finance industries. The company was founded in 2000 and is based in Wuhan, China.
