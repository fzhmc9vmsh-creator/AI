## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Jiangsu Xinquan Automotive Trim Co.,Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, designs, develops, manufactures, and sells automotive interior and exterior trim components and molds in China and internationally. The company offers instrument panel, overhead filing cabinet, door trim, column trim, drainage channel cover, bumper, and seat assemblies. It imports and exports various products and technologies products. The company was founded in 2001 and is headquartered in Changzhou, China.
