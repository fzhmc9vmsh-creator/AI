## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Sany Renewable Energy Co.,Ltd. engages in the research and development, manufacture, and sale of wind turbines and generators in China. The company operates through five segments: The Wind Turbine Manufacturing; The wind power services; Wind farm development; Power Plant Products Sales; and Others segments. It engages in the research and development, manufacturing and sales, maintenance and transportation services of wind turbines and their components; and provides wind power-related consulting and wind farm construction, as well as engages in wind farm development and operation. The company is also involved in development and sales of wind farms. In addition, it develops, manages, and operates wind farms. The company was founded in 2008 and is based in Beijing, China.
