## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Dalipal Holdings Limited, an investment holding company, supplies application equipment for energy development in the People's Republic of China and internationally. It develops, manufactures, and sells oil and gas pipes, new energy and special seamless and steel pipes, and other products. Dalipal Holdings Limited was founded in 1998 and is headquartered in Cangzhou, the People's Republic of China.
