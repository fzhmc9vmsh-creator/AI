## 事業の説明(yfinance)

China Education Group Holdings Limited, an investment holding company, operates private higher and secondary vocational education institutions in Mainland China and Australia. The company offers junior college diploma, bachelor's degree, master's degree, continuing education programs, and vocational education programs. It also provides educational consultancy services. China Education Group Holdings Limited was founded in 1989 and is headquartered in Central, Hong Kong.
