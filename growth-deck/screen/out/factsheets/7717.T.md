## 事業の説明(yfinance)

V Technology Co., Ltd. engages in the development, manufacture, sale, and service of equipment for flat panel displays (FPDs) and semiconductors in Japan. The company offers manufacturing and inspection equipment for the inspection, repair, and measurement of FPDs. It also provides photomask equipment; and parts and materials for manufacturing FPDs, as well as product maintenance and inspection service and maintenance parts, and offers equipment upgrade services. In addition, the company is involved in the semiconductor equipment business. The company was incorporated in 1997 and is headquartered in Yokohama, Japan.
