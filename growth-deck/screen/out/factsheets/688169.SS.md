## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Beijing Roborock Technology Co., Ltd. engages design, research and development, production, and sales of intelligent sweeping robots in China. It offers robot and wet dry vacuums; cordless vacuum cleaners; robotic lawn mowers; and accessories. The company was incorporated in 2014 and is headquartered in Beijing, China.
