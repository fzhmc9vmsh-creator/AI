## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Fixstars Corporation operates as a software company in Japan and internationally. It operates through Solution Business and Saas Business segments. The company provides embedded acceleration, image processing algorithm development, distributed parallel system development, GPU acceleration, FPGA systems development, quantum computing, AI and deep learning, automotive software development, and firmware development for flash memory solutions. It also offers Fixstars Amplify, a cloud platform for application development of various Ising machines; Smart Opinion, a telemedical platform for AI-based breast cancer analysis, aiding early detection and promoting women's health; CloneTracker, a tool for managing duplicate code; and Fixstars AIBooster, a GPU cloud service for the development and operations of generative AI and large language models. In addition, the company is involved in the multicore processor related business. Fixstars Corporation was incorporated in 2002 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
