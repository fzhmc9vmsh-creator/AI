## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Chengdu Haoneng Technology Co., Ltd. engages in the research, development, production, and sale of automotive transmission system components and aerospace parts in China and internationally. It offers synchronizer rings, such as copper synchronizer rings, stamped steel rings, and precision forged steel rings; gear hubs, gear sleeves, coupling gears, and synchronizer assemblies. The company also sells aircraft nose, fuselage, wings, and tail section for use in various types of military aircraft, civil aircraft and unmanned aerial vehicles. In addition, it engages in robot components business. The company was founded in 2006 and is based in Chengdu, China.
