## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Tellhow Sci-Tech Co., Ltd. provides military equipment and intelligent power in China and internationally. The company offers communication command system integration and information vehicles; satellite navigation and vehicle electronic information system products; photoelectric imaging and precision tracking and measuring products; combat command support systems; military power station and mechatronics products; military air conditioner products; ammunition and weapon systems; fuses; and rocket/missile engine charges. It also provides energy interconnection solutions; power grid products; intelligent emergency power products; and power distribution products. In addition, it engages in consulting, design, financing, construction, operation, and maintenance of projects, including hydropower, photovoltaic and other distributed energy power stations, and power distribution systems. The company was founded in 1996 and is headquartered in Nanchang, China.
