## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Guoquan Food (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. operates as a home meal products company in Mainland China. It offers ready-to-eat, ready-to-heat, and ready-to-cook meals; at-home hotpot and prepared ingredients; condiments and sauce products; drinks; fresh food; and western food and snacks.The company also provides barbecue products, solo-dining meals, hotpot soup base products, meatballs, shrimp paste, meat, vegetables, and pots and grills. In addition, it offers supply chain management, information technology, and enterprise management services. Further, the company processes and sells aquatic products. It sells its products through a network of stores under the Guoquan Shihui brand name, as well as through online channels. Guoquan Food (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. was founded in 2015 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
