## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Guangdong Dtech Technology Co., Ltd. engages in the research and development, production, and sale of tools, materials, and equipment in China and internationally. The company offers milling cutters, blades, and drill bits; and stainless steel pressure plate cleaning brushes, cutting force grinding brushes, non-woven fabric grinding brushes, ceramic grinding brushes, nylon grinding brushes, grinding circular sand belt mold brushes, diamond brush dressing plates, and golden finger grinding wheels. It also provides CNC tool grinders, grinding machines, CNC tap thread grinders, automatic sandblasting passivation machines, abrasive wheel CNC forming machines, cathodic arcs, hot filaments, automatic image measuring instruments, and intelligent drilling needle library systems. Its products are used in the PCB, metalworking, semiconductor, smart warehousing, light control, and vacuum coating industries. The company was founded in 2013 and is headquartered in Dongguan, China. Guangdong Dtech Technology Co., Ltd. operates as a subsidiary of Guangdong Taiding Holding Co., Ltd.
