## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Shenzhen Hopewind Electric Co., Ltd. provides green power solutions worldwide. It engages in the research and development, manufacture, sale, and service of energy and electrical fields. The company also provides solar inverters, including PV grid-tied inverters and turnkey solutions; energy storage systems, such as power conversion system and PCS turkey station products; and wind power devices, such as doubly-fed and full power converters, and pitch control systems. In addition, it offers hydrogen energy products, including hydrogen energy, DC/DC Converter, AC/DC single-stage topology, AC/DC+DC/DC two-stage topology; medium voltage, low-voltage engineering, and low-voltage VFD products; other station-level devices; remote intelligent operation and maintenance (O&M) system, energy management system; and STATCOM products, as well as hopeCloud remote intelligent maintenance cloud service platform; hopeView converter network monitoring system; hopeScan mobile assistant; hopeGate intelligent maintenance data acquisition system; hopeFarm wind farm remote monitoring system. The company provides solutions to PV, energy storage, wind power, hydrogen energy, power quality, energy management, intelligent O&M, and power consumption and other industries. Shenzhen Hopewind Electric Co., Ltd. was founded in 2007 and is headquartered in Shenzhen, China.
