## 事業の説明(yfinance)

GA technologies Co., Ltd. operates a real estate brokerage platform. The company offers Renosy, an AI real estate investment platform; Shenjumiaosuan, a platform that connects Japanese real estate brokerage companies with investors living in the Greater China region; dearlife, a rental estates platform for expatriates in Thailand; ITANDI BB, a site between real estate agents that allows search for properties and make reservations for previews and move-in applications; ITANDI Rental Brokerage, a sales support service for real estate rental brokerage; and ITANDI Sales PropoCloud, a sales support system for real estate sales and purchase brokerage companies. It also provides Urban luxury real estate platform; BtoB SaaS PropTech product series; and industry-specific m and a brokerage counseling. The company was incorporated in 2013 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
