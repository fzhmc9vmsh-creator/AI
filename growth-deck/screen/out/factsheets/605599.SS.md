## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Beijing Caishikou Department Store Co.,Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the gold and jewelry retail business in China. The company offers gold ornaments, precious metal investment products, precious metal cultural products, and diamond and jade jewelry under the Caibai Jewelry, Caibai Heritage, and Caibai Joyful Time brands. It also provides value-added services, including jewelry customization, personalized design, jewelry consultation, and trade-in programs. In addition, the company engages in arts and crafts; and gold recycling activities. It sells its products through offline direct-sales stores, online e-commerce channels, and banking channels. Beijing Caishikou Department Store Co.,Ltd. was incorporated in 1994 and is headquartered in Beijing, China.
