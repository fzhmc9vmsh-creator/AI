## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Green Tea Group Limited operates casual Chinese restaurants in Mainland China. The company operates restaurants under the Green Tea, Mang Gang Le, and LongJing brand names. Green Tea Group Limited was founded in 2004 and is headquartered in Hangzhou, China. The company operates as a subsidiary of Time Sonic Investments Limited.
