## 事業の説明(yfinance)

CGN Mining Company Limited engages in the development and trading of natural uranium resources to nuclear power plants. It operates through Natural Uranium Trading and Other Investments segments. The company also invests in natural uranium resources. It operates in the People's Republic of China, Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Europe, the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom. The company was founded in 1998 and is headquartered in Wan Chai, Hong Kong. CGN Mining Company Limited is a subsidiary of China Uranium Development Company Limited.
