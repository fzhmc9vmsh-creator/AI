## 事業の説明(yfinance)

SRE Holdings Corporation engages in the AI cloud and consulting, and real estate businesses in Japan. It offers packaged cloud tools and algorithms based on modules that provides machine learning and other advanced technologies to enhance operations in the real estate industry. The company is also involved in real estate brokerage and consulting services; and smart home services developing and selling condominiums. It serves real estate, finance, IT, healthcare, and other sectors. The company was formerly known as Sony Real Estate Corporation and changed its name to SRE Holdings Corporation in June 2019. SRE Holdings Corporation was incorporated in 2014 and is headquartered in Minato, Japan.
