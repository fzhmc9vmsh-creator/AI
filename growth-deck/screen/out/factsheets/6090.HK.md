## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Butong Group through its subsidiaries, engages in the designing, manufacturing, and selling of nursery products. The company offers travel gears, such as strollers, car seats and baby carriers; sleep gears including cribs, pajamas and pillows; feeding gears consisting of highchairs and tablewares; baby care products comprising diapers and wipes. It also engages in e-commerce business; sales and marketing; product procurement, sales, design, and research and development; marketing and promotion; and aftersales services. The company was founded in 2018 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
