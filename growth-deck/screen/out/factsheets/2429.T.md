## 事業の説明(yfinance)

World Holdings Co., Ltd. engages in human resources and education, information and telecommunications, real estate, and agricultural park businesses in Japan. The company offers subcontracting, business process outsourcing, temporary staffing services, research and development, manufacturing, electromechanical design development, information technology system design development, and medical and biotechnology research and development services. It is also involved in the planning, development, and sale of condominiums; renovation of condominiums and detached houses; rental property brokerage and real estate brokerage businesses; development, manufacturing, sale, and rental of prefabricated houses; real estate fund management; and the management and collection of receivables. In addition, the company engages in the operation of mobile phone stores and call centers; management of agricultural parks; electronics manufacturing services; power supply businesses; and electronic device repair services. The company was formerly known as WORLD INTEC Co., Ltd. The company was incorporated in 1993 and is headquartered in Fukuoka City, Japan.
