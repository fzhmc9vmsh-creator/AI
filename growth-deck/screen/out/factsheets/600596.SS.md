## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Zhejiang Xinan Chemical Industrial Group Co.,Ltd engages in the development, manufacture, and sale of chemical products for plant protection in China and internationally. The company provides crop protection system of seeds and seedlings, herbicides, insecticides, fungicides, crop nutrition and other products; silicon-based materials, such as silicon metal; cyclics and polymers; silicone rubbers and additives; sealants and adhesives, modified silicone oil and emulsion; silanes; and silicone resins, as well as silicon based solutions; and phosphorus based new materials, such as phosphorus trichloride, phosphorus oxychloride, TCPP, TEP and other products. Its products are used in electric power communication, rail transportation and automobile, medical and health care, consumer electronics and other fields. Zhejiang Xinan Chemical Industrial Group Co.,Ltd was founded in 1965 and is headquartered in Jiande, China.
