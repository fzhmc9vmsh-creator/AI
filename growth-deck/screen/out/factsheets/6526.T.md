## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Socionext Inc. designs, develops, manufactures, and sells system-on-chip (SoC), and solutions/services centering on SoC worldwide. The company offers application specific standard products in the areas of radar sensor, IoT communication, automotive, video processing, and demodulator LSI. It also provides custom SoC solutions, such as design technology, development support, CPU subsystem, IP macro, and manufacturing technology services. The company serves automotive, data center and networking, and smart devices/industrial sectors. Socionext Inc. was incorporated in 2014 and is headquartered in Yokohama, Japan.
