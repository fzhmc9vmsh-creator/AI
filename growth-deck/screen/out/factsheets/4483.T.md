## 事業の説明(yfinance)

JMDC Inc. provides medical statistics data services in Japan. It operates through two segments: Health Big Data and Telemedicine. The company offers big data services for medical field, including WEB tool, analytic, raw data provision, and publication support services; data provision and aggregation, consulting, and solution development services; supporting health business for health insurance unions and local government health services; health data platform; and medical institution support services. It also provides JMDC Claims Database, an epidemiological receipt database that has accumulated receipts, including inpatient, outpatient, and dispensing, as well as medical examination data received from multiple health insurance associations. It serves doctors, patients, medical institutions, insurers, pharmaceutical companies, life and non-life insurance companies, and research institutions. JMDC Inc. was incorporated in 2002 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan. JMDC Inc. is a subsidiary of OMRON Corporation.
