## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Beijing Chunlizhengda Medical Instruments Co., Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the research, development, production, and sale of surgical implants, instruments, and related products in the People's Republic of China and internationally. The company offers orthopedic medical devices and tissue repair products, including spine, trauma, joint, sports medicine, and bone repair material implants; surgical instrumentation products; platelet-rich plasma preparation kits; and closed wound negative pressure drainage kits. It also provides surgical robots; joint, spinal, sports medicine, trauma, oral care, and endoscopic products; orthopedic power products; and platelet-rich plasma preparation system products, as well as alloy materials and surgical powering equipment. The company exports its products to approximately 68 countries. The company was formerly known as Chunli Limited and changed its name to Beijing Chunlizhengda Medical Instruments Co., Ltd. in September 2010. Beijing Chunlizhengda Medical Instruments Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1998 and is headquartered in Beijing, China.
