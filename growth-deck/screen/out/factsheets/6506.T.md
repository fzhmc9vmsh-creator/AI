## 事業の説明(yfinance)

YASKAWA Electric Corporation engages in manufacturing and sales of servo motors, AC drives and industrial robots. It also provides installation, maintenance, modification and update, and recovery support services. In addition, the company develops, designs, and produces industrial system electrical products for steel-making and other large plants; offers PV inverters, control equipment for small-scale power generation, and motor drive system for energy saving and creation; and logistic services. Further, it provides i3-Mechatronics for smart factories. The company's products are used in automotive, food and agriculture, social infrastructure, environmental energy, biomedical, and semiconductor and electronic components, including smartphones. The company was founded in 1915 and is headquartered in Kitakyushu, Japan.
