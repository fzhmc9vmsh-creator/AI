## 事業の説明(yfinance)

GoodWe Technologies Co., Ltd. engages in the research, manufacture, and sale of photovoltaic (PV) inverters and energy storage solutions in China and internationally. The company provides residential, commercial and industry, utility photovoltaic, photovoltaic building solutions; residential systems; and heat pump products. It also offers residential grid-tied inverters, commercial and industry-tied inverters, utility, residential energy storage, commercial and industry energy storage products. In addition, the company provides batteries, EV chargers, software and accessories, and PV building materials. Further, it offers energy storage products, such as split-phase, AC-coupled retrofit, and hybrid inverters. GoodWe Technologies Co., Ltd. was founded in 2010 and is headquartered in Suzhou, China.
