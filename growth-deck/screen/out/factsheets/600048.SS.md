## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Poly Developments and Holdings Group Co., Ltd. engages in the investment, development, and management of real estate properties in China and internationally. It is also involved in the new home sales agency, digital marketing, existing home brokerage, and channel distribution activities; architectural design business; operation of convention and exhibitions; private equity fund management business; investment in healthcare; and silk business. In addition, the company operates and manages shopping malls, hotels, tourist spots, and diversified urban properties, as well as golf courses, theme parks, natural ecological landscape resorts, cultural tourism destinations, and tourism real estate properties. Further, it provides property services, as well as long-term and short-term apartment rental services. Poly Developments and Holdings Group Co., Ltd. was founded in 1992 and is headquartered in Guangzhou, China.
