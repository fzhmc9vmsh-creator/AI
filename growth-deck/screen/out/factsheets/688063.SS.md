## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Pylon Technologies Co., Ltd. engages in the provision of energy storage systems (ESS) worldwide. The company offers energy storage battery systems, lightweight power battery systems, and battery cells for homes, industrial and commercial applications, power grids, communication base stations, vehicle-mounted energy storage, mobile energy storage, and lightweight power applications. It also provides residential ESS and BESS, Utility, and commercial and industrial energy storage systems; and Pylontech Cloud, a real-time system monitoring platform. Pylon Technologies Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2009 and is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
