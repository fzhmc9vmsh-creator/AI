## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Xiaocaiyuan International Holding Ltd., an investment holding company, engages in the restaurant business in the People's Republic of China. The company also provides catering delivery services and supply chain management solutions. It operates restaurants under the Xiaocaiyuan, Fuxinglou, and Caishou brand names. The company was founded in 2013 and is headquartered in Tongling, the People's Republic of China.
