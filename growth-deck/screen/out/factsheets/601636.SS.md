## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Zhuzhou Kibing Group Co.,Ltd engages in the research and development, manufacturing and sales of glass in China and internationally. It offers ceramic frit, tempered, laminated, insulating, triple silver low-e, reflective coated, online sunlight control coated, tinted, ultra clear, and clear float glass products. The company was formerly known as Zhuzhou Kibing Glass Group Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Zhuzhou Kibing Group Co.,Ltd in March 2010. Zhuzhou Kibing Group Co.,Ltd was founded in 2005 and is headquartered in Shenzhen, China.
