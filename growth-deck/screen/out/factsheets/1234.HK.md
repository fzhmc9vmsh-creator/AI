## 事業の説明(yfinance)

China Lilang Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells branded menswear and related accessories in the People's Republic of China. The company designs, sources, manufactures, and sells business and casual apparel for men under the LILANZ and LESS IS MORE brands. The company also offers management, brand management, trading, transactions, supply chain management, and storage services. It distributes its products online and through a retail and distribution network covering provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities. The company was founded in 1987 and is headquartered in Jinjiang, the People's Republic of China. China Lilang Limited is a subsidiary of Xiao Sheng International Limited.
