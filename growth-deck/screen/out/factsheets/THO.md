## 事業の説明(yfinance)

THOR Industries, Inc. manufactures and sells recreational vehicles (RVs), and related parts and accessories in the United States, Germany, rest of Europe, Canada, and internationally. It operates through three segments: North American Towable Recreational Vehicles; North American Motorized Recreational Vehicles; and European Recreational Vehicles. The company offers travel trailers and motorhomes; Class A, Class C and Class B motorhomes; and fifth wheels under the Airstream Classic, Globetrotter, International, Tradewind, Flying Cloud, Caravel, Bambi, World Traveler, Basecamp, Interstate, Atlas, Rangeline, Jay Flight, Jay Feather, Eagle, Pinnacle, Colt, Range Lite, 3X, Prowler, North Trail, Big Horn, Odyssey, Esteem, Emblem, Insignia, Montana, Springdale, Hideout, Sprinter, Arcadia, Bullet, Fuzion, Raptor, Reign, Passport, Sprout, Cougar, Coleman, Kodiak, Aspen Trail, Astoria, Voltage, Colorado, Sunset Trail, Zinger, Redwood, Classic, Sportsmen, Connect, Ridgeway, Durango, Durango Gold, Sportster X, Sienna, Sonic, Stratus, and SportTrek brands. It also provides gasoline and diesel Class A products under the ACE, Resonate, Hurricane, Windsport, Outlaw, Indigo, Luminate, Vegas, Axis, Rize, Scope, Sequence, Tellaro, Palladium, Talavera, Sanctuary, Tranquility, Four Winds, Chateau, Compass, Gemini, Magnitude, Omni, Inception, Pasadena, Inception HD, Pasadena SV, Palazzo, Riviera, Aria, Allegro, Allegro Bay, Allegro Breeze, Allegro Bus, Allegro Red, Byway, GH1, GH2, GT1, GT2, Midas, Open Trail, Phaeton, Wayfarer, and Zephyr. In addition, the company offers recreational vehicles; and motorized and towable recreational vehicles, including motorcaravans, campervans, urban vehicles, and caravan. Further, it sells RV-related products; and manufactures and sells aluminum extrusions and specialized component products. The company sells its products to independent and non-franchise dealers. THOR Industries, Inc. was founded in 1980 and is based in Elkhart, Indiana.
