## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Nippon Paper Industries Co., Ltd. engages in the production and sale of pulp and paper products in Japan, Oceania, Asia, North America, and internationally. It offers paper products, including printing and writing, business communication, wrapping, functional specialty, and converting papers; newsprints; household paper and professional products, which comprise facial and bath tissue, paper towels, pre-moistened and industrial-use wipes, and personal-care products; health care products; paper-based materials under the SHIELDPLUS brand; and paper straws. The company also provides kitchen products; kraft and dissolving pulps; cellulose nanofiber; agriculture products, such as seedlings; plywood and laminated lumber products; and other products comprising Cu-TOP copper ion-loaded cellulose and wet non-woven fabric, as well as a shelf-life-extending agent under the Wasa Green brand. In addition, it offers chemicals, including sulfite dissolving pulp, functional chemicals, lignin products, ribonucleic acid/yeast products, functional coating resin, and optical films for display devices. Further, the company is involved in the energy business and construction-related business. The company was incorporated in 1949 and is headquartered in Chiyoda, Japan.
