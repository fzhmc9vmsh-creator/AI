## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Fiberhome Telecommunication Technologies Co., Ltd. provides information and communication network products and solutions in China and internationally. It offers fixed network, wireless network, optical fiber and cable, marine network, digital power, transport network, access network, ODN, smart home, data communications, RAN, antenna, CPE, indoor and outdoor cable, telecommunication copper cable, overhead transmission, optical fibers, dry plant, marine cable, wet plant, data center, and site and solar energy. The company was founded in 1999 and is headquartered in Wuhan, China.
