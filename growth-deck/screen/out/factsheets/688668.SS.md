## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Dongguan Dingtong Precision Metal Co., Ltd. engages in the product research and development, production, and sales of communication connectors in China. It also offers automotive connector assemblies, precision mold, mold parts, and liquid cooling products. Dongguan Dingtong Precision Metal Co., Ltd. was formerly Dongguan Dingtong Precision Hardware Co., Ltd. The company was incorporated in 2003 and is headquartered in Dongguan, China.
