## 事業の説明(yfinance)

China Jinmao Holdings Group Limited engages in residential and integrated property development in Mainland China. It operates through five segments: Property Development, Property Investment, Hotel Operations, Jinmao Services, and Others. The Property Development segment develops residential and comprehensive properties. The Property Investment segment leases office and retail commercial premises. The Hotel Operations segment offers hotel accommodation services, and food and beverage. The Jinmao Services segment provides property management services and community value-added services, as well as value-added services to non-property owners. The Others segment offers design and decoration services. This segment is also involved in building technology and other services. The company was formerly known as Franshion Properties (China) Limited and changed its name to China Jinmao Holdings Group Limited in September 2015. China Jinmao Holdings Group Limited was incorporated in 2004 and is headquartered in Beijing, China.
