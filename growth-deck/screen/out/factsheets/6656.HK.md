## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Sigenergy Technology Co., Ltd develops and manufactures energy storage systems, inverters, and related smart energy solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial applications. It offers Sigenstor, 5-in-one energy storage systems; Sigen LoadHub, a backup power solution provides intelligent energy management and monitoring; Sigen backup switch, a plug and play backup solution; SigenMicro inverter; Sigen EV DC charging modules; and Sigen EV AC chargers. The company serves commercial and industrial clients, businesses, communities, the aquaculture sector, and residential clients. It operates in Asia-Pacific, Europe, Africa, Chinese Mainland, Middle East and Central Asia, Latin America, and Northern America. The company was founded in 2022 and is based in Shanghai, China.
