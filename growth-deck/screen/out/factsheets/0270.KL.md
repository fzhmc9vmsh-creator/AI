## 事業の説明(yfinance)

NationGate Holdings Berhad, an investment holding company, provides electronic manufacturing services. It is involved in the assembly and testing of electronic components and products to produce completed printed circuit boards, semi-finished subassemblies, and fully assembled electronic products, as well as semiconductor devices. The company also engages in the manufacture of servers, server racks, network switches, and other electronic components; warehousing, manufacturer, general carriers, agents, importers and exporters, wholesalers and retailers, and general trading activities; and provision of logistics services. It serves various industries, such as networking and telecommunications, data computing, industrial instrumentation, consumer electronics, automotive, semiconductor, and aerospace, as well as medical devices, internet of things, household electronic products, and analytical instrumentation devices. The company has operations in Malaysia, Singapore, Other Asia Pacific countries, the United States of America, Germany, Seychelles, Canada, France, Denmark, the United Kingdom, and internationally. NationGate Holdings Berhad was founded in 1999 and is headquartered in Perai, Malaysia.
