## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Kingfa Sci. & Tech. Co., Ltd. engages in the research, development, production, and sale of new chemical materials worldwide. The company offers modified plastics, recycled and biodegradable plastics, specialty engineering plastics, fiber-reinforced composites, light hydrocarbons and hydrogen energy, polypropylene resins, styrene resins, and medical and health polymer materials. Its products are used in automotive, home appliance, electronics, communications, new infrastructure, new energy, modern agriculture, modern logistics, rail transportation, aerospace, and high-end equipment industries. Kingfa Sci. & Tech. Co., Ltd. was founded in 1993 and is headquartered in Guangzhou, China.
