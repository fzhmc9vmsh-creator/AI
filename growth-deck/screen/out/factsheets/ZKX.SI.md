## 事業の説明(yfinance)

Ever Glory United Holdings Limited, an investment holding company, provides mechanical and electrical engineering services for the public and private sectors in Singapore. The company operates through the M&E Engineering Services and Property Development segments. It engages in the installation and maintenance of air conditioning and mechanical ventilation systems; electrical installations, transmission and distribution networks, power supply equipment, and airfield lighting systems; the installation of plumbing, sanitary, and gas systems for buildings; and fire protection alarm systems in infrastructure projects. The company provides technology solutions such as tailgating detection systems, lift monitoring systems, and bus stop information displays; infrastructure fire protection systems; critical power and smart infrastructure solutions to data centers, public infrastructure, and other commercial installation projects; and sustainable and smart energy solutions for electric vehicle charging systems and solar PV applications. It also designs, installs, and maintains electrical systems; installs and maintains street lighting systems, public car park systems, and airfield ground lighting systems; designs, manufactures, and manages low- and medium-voltage switchgears and switchboards; and invests in, constructs, and develops real estate properties. Ever Glory United Holdings Limited was incorporated in 2021 and is headquartered in Singapore.
