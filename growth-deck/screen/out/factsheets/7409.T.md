## 事業の説明(yfinance)

AeroEdge Co., Ltd. engages in the manufacturing and sale of aerospace engine parts in Japan, France, and internationally. The company also provides machining services, such as 3-axis and 5-axis machining, combined machining, grinding and polishing, additive manufacturing (AM), powder bed fusion, directed energy deposition, wire electrical discharge machining, 5-axis ultrasonic machining, water jet processing, grinding of cutting tools, nondestructive testing fluorescence penetrant inspection/digital x-ray inspection, 3D measurement and scan, and gears and spline processing. In addition, it offers AM prototype and consulting services; Arcam/GE Additive A2X and DMG MORI/Lasertech65 DED hybrid; and engineering services. The company was incorporated in 2015 and is headquartered in Ashikaga, Japan.
